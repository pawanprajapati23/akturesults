What is AKTU Back Paper?

AKTU back paper refers to the supplementary or carry-over examination conducted for students who failed in one or more subjects in their regular semester exams. Students can appear for these exams to clear their backlogs and complete their degree requirements.

📊 Types of Back Papers

  • Backlog: Failed subjects from previous semesters
  • Carry Over: Subjects carried forward to next semester
  • Supplementary: Re-examination for failed subjects
  • Improvement: Re-attempt to improve marks

📝 How to Check Back Paper Result

Method 1: Using One View (Recommended)

  1. Visit AKTU One View portal
  2. Enter your enrollment number
  3. Click "Submit" to view your result
  4. All semester results including back papers will be displayed

Method 2: Using ERP Portal

  1. Login to AKTU ERP at erp.aktu.ac.in
  2. Go to "Examination" section
  3. Click on "View Result"
  4. Select the semester and view back paper marks

💰 Back Paper Exam Form & Fees

Important Details

  • Exam Form Fee: ₹300 per subject (approximately)
  • Form Submission: Online through ERP portal
  • Payment Mode: Online (Debit/Credit card, Net Banking)
  • Last Date: Check AKTU official notifications

📅 Back Paper Exam Schedule

AKTU conducts back paper/supplementary exams usually:

  • After each semester exam (within 2-3 months)
  • Along with regular semester exams
  • Special supplementary exams (announced separately)

💡 Important Points

  • Students can carry forward a maximum number of backlogs (check AKTU rules)
  • Final year students must clear all backlogs to receive degree
  • Back paper results are declared along with regular results
  • Marks obtained in back papers are mentioned separately on marksheet
  • Fill exam forms within the deadline to avoid late fees
  • Keep checking ERP portal for back paper notifications
