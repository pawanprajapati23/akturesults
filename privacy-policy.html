Privacy Policy

How We Protect Your Information

Effective Date: February 28, 2026

Introduction

At AKTU Results (akturesults.in), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website.

Important: We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers unless you voluntarily provide them through contact forms.

Information We Collect

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we automatically collect certain information through:

  • Google Analytics: We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website
  • Browser Information: Browser type, device type, operating system
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent on pages, click patterns
  • IP Address: Your IP address for analytics and security purposes
  • Cookies: Small data files stored on your device (see Cookies section below)

Voluntarily Provided Information

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily provide:

  • Contact forms (if you choose to contact us)
  • Comments or feedback (if available)
  • Newsletter subscriptions (if you opt-in)

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Website Improvement: To analyze user behavior and improve website functionality
  • Content Optimization: To understand which content is most valuable to users
  • Technical Issues: To identify and fix technical problems
  • Security: To protect against malicious activities and spam
  • Communication: To respond to your inquiries (only if you contact us)
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with legal obligations

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic website functionality
  • Analytics Cookies: Google Analytics cookies to track website usage
  • Advertising Cookies: Google AdSense cookies for personalized advertisements

You can control cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling cookies may affect website functionality.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect information:

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to track website traffic and user behavior. Google Analytics may collect:

  • Device and browser information
  • Geographic location (city/country level)
  • Pages visited and time spent
  • Referring websites

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy

Google AdSense

We display advertisements through Google AdSense. Google may use cookies to show personalized ads based on your browsing history.

Learn more: Google Ads Privacy

Opt-out: Google Ad Settings

External Links

Our website contains links to external websites including:

  • Official AKTU portals (aktu.ac.in, erp.aktu.ac.in)
  • Educational resources
  • Reference materials

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external websites. Please review their privacy policies separately.

Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect information:

  • HTTPS encryption for secure data transmission
  • Regular security updates and monitoring
  • Limited access to any collected data
  • Secure hosting infrastructure

However, no method of internet transmission is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

We retain collected data as follows:

  • Analytics Data: Retained by Google Analytics according to their retention policies
  • Contact Information: Retained only as long as necessary to respond to inquiries
  • Cookies: Expire based on cookie type (session or persistent)

Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for students and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 years of age without parental consent. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us to have it removed.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your data:

  • Access: Request information about data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data
  • Opt-Out: Opt-out of analytics and advertising cookies
  • Data Portability: Request a copy of your data in a portable format

Do Not Track

Some browsers have "Do Not Track" features. Currently, we do not respond to Do Not Track signals. However, you can disable cookies and tracking through your browser settings.

International Users

Our website is primarily intended for users in India. If you are accessing from outside India, your information may be transferred to and processed in India or other countries where our service providers operate.

Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date." Your continued use of the website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to exercise your data rights, please contact us through our Contact Page or email us at diplomawithbtech@gmail.com

Summary: We respect your privacy. We only collect minimal, anonymous data through Google Analytics and AdSense to improve our website. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties (except service providers like Google). You can opt-out of tracking through browser settings.
