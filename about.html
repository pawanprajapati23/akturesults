About AKTU Results

Your Trusted AKTU Information Portal

Who We Are

AKTU Results is a comprehensive information portal dedicated to providing accurate and timely information about Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU) to students, parents, and educators.

We are an independent platform created to help students navigate through their academic journey at AKTU by providing easy access to results, exam schedules, admission details, and other important academic information. Whether you need to check your ERP login or find syllabus information, we've got you covered.

Our Mission

To empower AKTU students with quick, reliable, and comprehensive academic information, making their university experience smoother and more informed.

What We Offer

  • Result Information: Direct links and guides to check AKTU results across all semesters and courses. Access One View results, CGPA calculator, and back paper results
  • Exam Updates: Latest exam schedules, date sheets, admit cards, and examination guidelines
  • Admission Guidance: Complete information about AKTU admissions, counselling, cutoffs, and college selection
  • Academic Resources: Syllabus, study materials, previous year papers, and calculation tools like grade calculator & attendance calculator
  • College Information: Details about AKTU affiliated colleges across Uttar Pradesh including Lucknow, Noida, and Kanpur
  • Placement Data: Company information, preparation tips, and placement statistics

Why Choose Us

✓ Accurate Information: We only provide verified information and official links to ensure reliability

✓ Regular Updates: Our content is continuously updated with latest notifications and news updates

✓ User-Friendly: Clean, fast, and mobile-responsive design for easy access on any device

✓ Comprehensive: All AKTU-related information in one place - from results to placements

✓ No Login Required: Access most information without any registration, including One View results

Important Disclaimer

AKTU Results is an independent information portal and is NOT officially affiliated with Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU) or any government body.

We provide information for educational purposes only. For official and legally binding information, please always refer to the official AKTU website at aktu.ac.in
