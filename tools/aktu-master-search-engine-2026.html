📜 Complete AKTU Student Examination & Admission Guide 2026

Welcome to the official AKTU Master Knowledge & Directory Hub. This portal provides verified, 1-click solutions for Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU Lucknow) students across Uttar Pradesh.

1. AKTU One View Result Portal

Students can check their semester results on the AKTU One View portal using their University Roll Number without needing ERP password authentication.

2. Official CGPA to Percentage Formula

Under AKTU ordinances, CGPA is converted to percentage using the formula: Percentage = (CGPA - 0.75) × 10. For example, 8.50 CGPA equals 77.50%.

3. Grace Marks & Carryover Rules

Candidates scoring at least 35% in external theory (25/70) but short of 40% combined total qualify for up to 7 Grace Marks per year. Carryover Paper (COP) examination fee is ₹1,000 per paper.
