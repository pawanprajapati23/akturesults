📜 Official AKTU Internal & External Passing Criteria Rules

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU) enforces a dual-condition passing rule for all B.Tech, MBA, MCA, B.Pharm, and B.Arch theory subjects under CBCS ordinance:

Parameter Standard 70-Mark Theory Paper Old Scheme 100-Mark Theory Practical / Lab Exam (50 Marks)
Internal Sessional Max 30 Marks 50 Marks 50 Marks
Minimum External Needed (35%) 25 Marks out of 70 35 Marks out of 100 25 Marks out of 50
Minimum Total Needed (40%) 40 Marks out of 100 60 Marks out of 150 50 Marks out of 100

🎁 AKTU 7-Mark Grace Rule (PWG)

If a candidate passes in external theory baseline (25/70) but falls short of total 40 marks by up to 7 marks across the academic year, AKTU awards Grace Marks automatically. Result is marked as PWG (Passed With Grace).
