📜 AKTU Official Grace Marks & Pass Criteria Rules

  • Grace Marks Rule: AKTU awards up to 7 grace marks per academic year (or 10 marks in final year) if a student is failing in 1 or 2 subjects. Marked as PWG (Passed With Grace) in One View.
  • PCP Full Form: Promoted with Carry Over Papers (Student is promoted to next year but has back paper in previous semester).
  • Division Criteria:
    • First Division with Honours: CGPA ≥ 7.50 without any back paper throughout course.
    • First Division: CGPA ≥ 6.50
    • Second Division: CGPA ≥ 5.00
