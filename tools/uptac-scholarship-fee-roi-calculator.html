🎓 Understanding Fee Waiver (FW) Seats in UPTAC Counselling

Fee Waiver (FW) seats are 5% supernumerary seats created by AICTE and allocated through UPTAC (formerly UPSEE) counselling. Here are the core rules:

  • 100% Tuition Exemption: Students admitted under FW category pay ₹0 tuition fee for all 4 years.
  • Merit Cum Means Basis: Allocated strictly according to UPTAC CRL / Category rank to candidates whose annual family income is below ₹6,00,000 (6 Lakhs).
  • Mandatory Expenses: FW students only pay annual hostel/mess fees, university exam fees, and student activity charges.

🏛️ UP Scholarship (Saksham Portal) Income & Refund Eligibility Rules

The Uttar Pradesh Social Welfare Department provides post-matric scholarship and fee reimbursement via the scholarship.up.gov.in portal under strict income parameters:

  • Income Cap (General / OBC / EWS / Minority): Family income must not exceed ₹2,00,000 per annum (2.0 Lakhs).
  • Income Cap (SC / ST): Family income must not exceed ₹2,50,000 per annum (2.5 Lakhs).
  • Private College Refund Cap: For private self-financed colleges, tuition fee reimbursement is capped at a maximum of ₹56,600 per year for B.Tech courses.
  • Maintenance Allowance: Eligible hostellers receive up to ₹12,000/year, while day scholars receive ₹6,000/year as maintenance allowance.
  • 75% Attendance Requirement: Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance recorded on the biometric / ERP portal to receive scholarship funds.

💻 Step-by-Step Guide: How to Apply for UP Scholarship on Saksham Portal

Follow these steps to claim fee reimbursement and scholarship allowance after securing admission through UPTAC or direct management quota:

Step 1: Registration on scholarship.up.gov.in

Visit the official portal scholarship.up.gov.in. Click on 'Student' -> 'Registration' -> Select 'Postmatric Other Than Inter (Fresh)' based on your category.

Step 2: Fill Personal & Academic Details

Enter your Aadhaar number, high school roll number, bank account details (seeded with Aadhaar NPCI mapper), and UPTAC admission allotment letter number.

Step 3: Upload Mandatory Documents

Upload scanned copies of Income Certificate (issued by Tehsil/SDM), Caste Certificate, Domicile Certificate (UP Domicile mandatory for general category), Fee Receipt, and College ID Card.

Step 4: College Verification & Physical Form Submission

Print the final application draft and submit the hard copy along with attached self-attested documents to your college scholarship department for online verification and forward approval.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (UPTAC Scholarship & ROI 2026)

Q1. Can a student enjoy both Fee Waiver (FW) seat and UP Scholarship tuition refund simultaneously?

No. Since a Fee Waiver seat already waives 100% of the tuition fee, the Social Welfare Department will not disburse tuition fee reimbursement. However, eligible FW students from SC/ST or low-income categories may still receive the annual maintenance allowance.

Q2. What if my annual family income is ₹2.1 Lakhs and I belong to OBC category?

Unfortunately, the UP Saksham portal automatically rejects applications for OBC/General candidates whose income certificate shows family income above ₹2,00,000 per annum. SC/ST candidates are eligible up to ₹2,50,000 per annum.

Q3. How is the payback period / ROI calculated?

Payback period represents the number of months of post-tax salary required to recover your 4-year net out-of-pocket college expenditure. A payback period of less than 12-18 months indicates an exceptional return on educational investment.

Q4. Why is the tuition fee refund capped at ₹56,600 for private AKTU colleges?

The Uttar Pradesh state government has fixed the maximum tuition reimbursement rate for private professional institutions at ₹56,600 per student per year. Even if your college charges ₹1.4 Lakhs annual tuition, the state government refund will not exceed ₹56,600.
