🔗 Go to AKTU ERP Portal

What is AKTU ERP Portal?

AKTU ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) Portal is the official student portal of Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University. It provides students with access to academic records, results, attendance, exam forms, fee payment, and many other important services.

📌 Important Information

Your enrollment number is your username, and your default password is usually your date of birth (DDMMYYYY format). Change your password after first login for security.

📝 Step-by-Step Login Process

1 Visit Official Portal

Open your web browser and visit erp.aktu.ac.in or click the login button at the top of this page.

2 Enter Your Credentials

  • Username: Enter your enrollment number (e.g., 2201234567890)
  • Password: Enter your password (default: date of birth in DDMMYYYY format)
  • Example: If DOB is 15th Jan 2005, password is 15012005

3 Enter Captcha Code

Type the captcha code shown in the image. Make sure to enter it correctly (case-sensitive).

4 Click Login Button

After entering all details correctly, click on the "Login" button to access your dashboard.

5 Change Password (First Time)

For security reasons, change your default password immediately after your first login. Use a strong password with letters, numbers, and special characters.

🔧 Troubleshooting Common Issues

❌ Problem: "Invalid Username or Password"

  • Double-check your enrollment number (no spaces)
  • Verify date of birth format: DDMMYYYY
  • Try default password: date of birth
  • Clear browser cache and cookies
  • Use a different browser (Chrome, Firefox recommended)

❌ Problem: "Captcha Error"

  • Captcha is case-sensitive, type carefully
  • Refresh the page if captcha is not visible
  • Try clicking the refresh icon next to captcha

❌ Problem: "Portal Not Opening / Server Down"

  • Check your internet connection
  • Try accessing during off-peak hours
  • Clear browser cache and try again
  • Wait for some time and retry (server may be busy)
  • Try using mobile data instead of WiFi

❌ Problem: "Forgot Password"

  • Click on "Forgot Password" link on login page
  • Enter enrollment number and registered email/mobile
  • Follow instructions sent to your email/SMS
  • If not working, contact your college administration

💡 Important Tips

  • Use Updated Browser: Use latest version of Chrome, Firefox, or Edge
  • Avoid Peak Hours: Access portal during early morning or late evening for better speed
  • Keep Credentials Safe: Never share your password with anyone
  • Regular Login: Login regularly to stay updated with notifications
  • Update Contact Details: Keep your email and mobile number updated
  • Save Important Documents: Download and save marksheets immediately
  • Check Email: AKTU sends important updates via email

🔒 Security Tips

  • Change default password immediately after first login
  • Use strong password with mix of letters, numbers, and symbols
  • Don't save password on public/shared computers
  • Always logout after completing your work

📞 Need Help?

If you're still facing issues with ERP login:

  • Contact your college IT department
  • Visit AKTU examination cell
  • Call AKTU helpline numbers (available on official website)
  • Send email to AKTU support with your enrollment number
