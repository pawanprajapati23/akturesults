Rules for Transferring NPTEL MOOCs Credits to AKTU ERP

  • Course Overlap Prohibition: The selected NPTEL course MUST NOT be part of your mandatory regular curriculum syllabus (e.g. if DBMS is already in your 4th sem syllabus, you cannot claim Honours credit for NPTEL DBMS).
  • Passing Benchmark: You must score at least 40% to 50% (Elite/Silver certificate) in the final proctored NPTEL exam.
  • Credit Submission Deadline: Submit your NPTEL e-certificate to your college Registrar/Department HOD before the commencement of 8th-semester final exams for ERP credit mapping.
