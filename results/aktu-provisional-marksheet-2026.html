How to Access Your Results

Follow these steps to check your AKTU examination results:

  • Step 1: Visit the official AKTU One View portal
  • Step 2: Enter your Enrollment Number (Roll Number)
  • Step 3: Enter Date of Birth in DD-MM-YYYY format
  • Step 4: Complete the captcha verification
  • Step 5: Click on Search or Submit button
  • Step 6: View your detailed result with marks and grades
  • Step 7: Download or save the result for future use

Important Information

  • Always use official AKTU websites to check results

Common Issues & Solutions

Server Not Working?

  • Try different browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Edge)
  • Clear browser cache and cookies
  • Use incognito/private mode
  • Switch between WiFi and mobile data
  • Check during off-peak hours

Forgot Enrollment Number?

Contact your college examination office or check your admit card, college ID card, or previous semester marksheets for your enrollment number.

Result Not Showing?

Ensure you're entering correct enrollment number and date of birth. If still not visible, results for your semester may not be declared yet. Check official AKTU notifications.
