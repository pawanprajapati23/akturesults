How to Check Results Online

Follow these simple steps to check your AKTU results:

  • Visit the official AKTU One View portal at erp.aktu.ac.in
  • Enter your Enrollment Number carefully
  • Enter your Date of Birth in DD-MM-YYYY format
  • Complete the captcha verification
  • Click on Search/Submit button
  • Your result will appear on screen with subject-wise marks
  • Download or take screenshot for future reference

Pass Criteria

To pass AKTU examinations, students must meet these requirements:

  • Minimum 40% marks in theory papers (internal + external combined)
  • Minimum 50% marks in practical/sessional work
  • Overall aggregate of 40% in all subjects
  • SGPA of 4.0 or above
  • Each subject must be cleared individually

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I check my result without enrollment number?

Unfortunately, enrollment number is mandatory to check AKTU results. Contact your college examination office to retrieve your enrollment number.

What if the result portal is not working?

Try accessing during off-peak hours (early morning or late night), clear browser cache, use different browser, or switch between WiFi and mobile data.

When will original marksheets be available?

Original marksheets are usually distributed by colleges 4-6 weeks after result declaration. Check with your examination office for exact dates.

Can I apply for revaluation?

Yes, you can apply for revaluation within 7-10 days of result declaration through the ERP portal. Fee is approximately ₹500-1000 per subject.
