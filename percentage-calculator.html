ℹ️ About AKTU CGPA Conversion

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU) uses a specific formula to convert CGPA to percentage. Unlike other universities that use a simple multiplication by 9.5 or 10, AKTU uses:

Percentage = (CGPA - 0.75) × 10

This means that a 7.5 CGPA in AKTU equals 67.5%, not 71.25% or 75% as in other universities. Always use the official AKTU formula for accurate results.
