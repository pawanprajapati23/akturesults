🍪 Cookie Policy

Last Updated: March 21, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, smartphone, tablet) when you visit our website. They help us provide you with a better experience by remembering your preferences and understanding how you use our site.

2. How We Use Cookies

akturesults.in uses cookies for the following purposes:

2.1 Essential Cookies

  • Session Management: Keep you logged in while browsing
  • Security: Protect against fraudulent activity
  • Functionality: Remember your preferences (language, theme)

2.2 Analytics Cookies

  • Google Analytics: Understand visitor behavior and site usage
  • Performance Tracking: Identify popular content and pages
  • User Flow: See how users navigate our website

2.3 Advertising Cookies

  • Google AdSense: Display relevant advertisements
  • Personalization: Show ads based on your interests
  • Ad Performance: Measure effectiveness of advertisements

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Cookie Type Purpose Duration
Session Cookies Temporary cookies deleted when you close browser Session only
Persistent Cookies Stay on device to remember preferences Up to 2 years
First-Party Cookies Set by akturesults.in directly Varies
Third-Party Cookies Set by Google Analytics, AdSense Varies

4. Specific Cookies We Use

4.1 Google Analytics Cookies

  • _ga: Distinguishes unique users (2 years)
  • _gid: Distinguishes users (24 hours)
  • _gat: Throttles request rate (1 minute)

4.2 Google AdSense Cookies

  • ID, DSID: Ad personalization
  • NID: Preferences and information
  • ANID: Display Google ads on non-Google sites

5. Managing Cookies

You have several options to manage or disable cookies:

5.1 Browser Settings

Most browsers allow you to:

  • View cookies stored on your device
  • Delete specific or all cookies
  • Block cookies from specific websites
  • Block all third-party cookies
  • Clear cookies when you close the browser

5.2 Browser-Specific Instructions

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies
  • Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies
  • Edge: Settings → Privacy → Cookies

5.3 Opt-Out Tools

  • Google Analytics: Opt-out Browser Add-on
  • Google Ads: Ad Settings
⚠️ Important: Disabling cookies may affect your experience on our website. Some features may not work properly without cookies enabled.
