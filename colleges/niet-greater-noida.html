Overview

Noida Institute of Engineering and Technology (commonly known as NIET) is one of the premier Private engineering institutions located in Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh. Affiliated with Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), Lucknow, NIET offers excellent infrastructure, highly qualified faculty, and an outstanding placement record.
