Department Overview

The Department of Computer Science & Engineering at Kamla Nehru Institute of Technology (KNIT), Sultanpur is one of Eastern Uttar Pradesh's most prestigious government engineering department CSE programmes, affiliated with AKTU. Known for producing exceptional talent that competes shoulder-to-shoulder with IIT and NIT graduates, KNIT Sultanpur CSE is a hidden gem for AKTU aspirants.

The department has earned national attention through its exceptional outcomes: Amazon has recruited a KNIT Sultanpur CSE graduate at 45 LPA, making it one of the highest packages from any government college in Eastern UP. Simultaneously, the department consistently produces 20+ GATE qualifiers per year, with students regularly getting selected to IITs and NITs for M.Tech programmes and PSU jobs at BSNL, NHPC, and other central PSUs.

Faculty at KNIT Sultanpur CSE have published in Scopus and SCI-indexed journals and guide PhD scholars in emerging areas like machine learning, cybersecurity, and distributed computing. The department's strong research culture, combined with a competitive programming club and placement cell, gives students an all-round technical advantage.

GATE Results & Research Publications

KNIT Sultanpur CSE is exceptionally strong in GATE outcomes — averaging 20–25 qualifiers per year from the CSE department alone. Successful GATE destinations include IIT Delhi, IIT Bombay, NIT Trichy, NIT Allahabad, and PSUs like BSNL and NHPC.

The department's faculty have authored 50+ research publications in Scopus-indexed journals (IEEE Transactions, Springer, Elsevier). Active research grants from DST and SERB fund ongoing projects in AI-driven healthcare, cybersecurity threat detection, and intelligent transport systems. Students co-author papers and present at IEEE-sponsored national conferences.
