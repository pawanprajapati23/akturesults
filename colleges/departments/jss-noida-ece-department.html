Department Overview

The Department of Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE) at JSS Academy of Technical Education, Noida is one of the flagship departments of this reputed institution, delivering a curriculum that seamlessly blends core electronics theory with modern communication systems, embedded design, and VLSI engineering.

JSS Noida's ECE department is equipped with three of the most sought-after specialized laboratories in the region: a state-of-the-art VLSI Design Lab, a comprehensive Embedded Systems Lab, and a fully operational Cisco Networking Academy Lab. These facilities make JSS Noida ECE graduates highly competitive for roles at semiconductor companies, embedded firms, and telecom corporates.

The department has registered consistent placement success, with a highest package of 57 LPA achieved by a JSS Noida ECE alumnus, and average packages ranging from 5–7 LPA for the core electronics stream. Core companies like Texas Instruments, Qualcomm, Samsung, Bosch, and Cisco have recruited from this department.

Lab Facilities

🔲 VLSI Design Lab

Cadence tools, Synopsys Design Compiler, Xilinx FPGA boards, ModelSim; RTL design, synthesis, and verification practicals.

🤖 Embedded Systems Lab

ARM Cortex-M microcontrollers, Arduino, Raspberry Pi, STM32 boards; RTOS programming and sensor interfacing projects.

🌐 Cisco Networking Academy Lab

Official Cisco Academy; Cisco Packet Tracer, CCNA/CCNP hardware, routers and switches for hands-on network configuration.

📡 Communication Systems Lab

AM/FM modulators, spectrum analyzers, digital oscilloscopes, software-defined radio (SDR) kits for Sem 4–6 experiments.

📊 DSP & Signal Processing Lab

MATLAB & Simulink, DSP development kits (TMS320), audio processing, filter design experiments.

📱 IoT & Wireless Lab

Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Zigbee, LoRa modules; end-to-end IoT prototyping from sensing to cloud visualization.

GATE & Higher Studies for ECE

ECE graduates from JSS Noida have multiple pathways:

  • GATE (EC paper) → M.Tech at IITs, NITs; PSU jobs at BEL, BSNL, ONGC, BARC
  • MS Abroad → MSEE/MSECE in USA (Cornell, UIUC, UT Austin) via GRE
  • Embedded/VLSI Certifications → Cadence, Synopsys; highly valued in chip design industry
  • Cisco CCNA/CCNP → Network engineering careers in India and globally
