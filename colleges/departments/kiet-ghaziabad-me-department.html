Department Overview

The Department of Mechanical Engineering at KIET Group of Institutions, Ghaziabad is among the most well-equipped ME departments in the NCR under AKTU, offering B.Tech Mechanical Engineering with a strong emphasis on design, manufacturing, thermodynamics, and advanced Industry 4.0 technologies.

What sets KIET ME apart is its forward-looking approach to mechanical engineering education. Beyond the standard AKTU curriculum, the department has established dedicated labs for CAD/CAM integration, CNC machining, and an Industry 4.0 Smart Manufacturing Lab — the last of which incorporates automation, robotics, machine learning for predictive maintenance, digital twins, and IIoT (Industrial Internet of Things) systems used in modern manufacturing plants.

KIET ME department is also a strong supporter of PSU recruitment pathways, with dedicated GATE coaching for Mechanical Engineering (ME paper). The department tracks and celebrates students who crack GATE and secure positions in BHEL, SAIL, ONGC, GAIL, NTPC, and other core engineering PSUs.

Laboratory Facilities

🖥️ CAD/CAM Lab

CATIA V5, SolidWorks, AutoCAD, ANSYS Workbench; 3D modelling, assembly design, simulation, and CNC G-code generation.

⚙️ CNC Machining Lab

CNC Turning Centre, CNC Milling Machine, Fanuc controller; students program and execute real metal-cutting operations.

🏭 Industry 4.0 Smart Lab

PLC/SCADA systems, industrial robots (ABB), digital twin simulation, predictive maintenance ML, IIoT dashboards.

🔥 Thermal Engineering Lab

IC Engine test rigs, bomb calorimeters, heat exchangers, refrigeration trainers; Thermodynamics and Heat Transfer experiments.

🔩 Strength of Materials Lab

UTM (Universal Testing Machine), hardness testers, torsion apparatus; material property measurement for Sem 4.

🌊 Fluid Mechanics Lab

Venturimeter, orifice meter, Pelton turbine trainer, pipe flow apparatus; all AKTU ME Sem 3 experiments covered.
