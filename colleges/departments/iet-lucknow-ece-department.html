Department Overview

The Department of Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE) at IET Lucknow carries the prestige of one of Uttar Pradesh's premier government engineering institutions. The department delivers rigorous training in electronics design, signal processing, communication systems, and VLSI engineering — areas at the cutting edge of global technology.

As a government college ECE department, IET Lucknow benefits from stable, highly experienced faculty — most PhD holders from IITs — a tradition of academic excellence, and a placement reputation that opens doors at semiconductor companies, defence research organizations, and telecom firms nationwide.

Three landmark laboratories define the ECE experience here: the VLSI Design Lab with Cadence and Xilinx FPGA tools, the DSP Lab with MATLAB and TI DSP kits, and the Communication Systems Lab equipped with modulation/demodulation hardware and spectrum analyzers.

Lab Facilities

🔲 VLSI Design Lab

Cadence Virtuoso, Xilinx Vivado, FPGA development boards; RTL-to-GDSII flow, synthesis and verification practicals.

📊 DSP Lab

MATLAB & Simulink, TI TMS320 DSP kits; digital filter design, FFT, convolution, audio processing experiments.

📡 Communication Systems Lab

AM/FM/PM modulators, digital modulation trainers, spectrum analyzers; analog & digital communication experiments.

🤖 Embedded Systems Lab

ARM Cortex-M4, STM32, Arduino, Raspberry Pi; RTOS programming and IoT prototyping projects.

⚡ Analog Electronics Lab

Op-Amp ICs, transistor characteristics, CRO, function generators; Sem 3–4 electronics circuit practicals.

🌊 Microwave & RF Lab

Microwave test bench, waveguide components, VNA network analyzer; Sem 6 microwave engineering experiments.
