Department Overview

The Computer Science & Engineering Department at KIET Group of Institutions, Ghaziabad is one of the most vibrant and technically active CSE departments in the NCR region under AKTU. Ranked consistently among the top AKTU private colleges, KIET's CSE department is celebrated not only for its academic rigor but also for its exceptional co-curricular and technical culture.

KIET CSE students are renowned across the state for their competitive programming achievements under the "KIET Coding Warriors" banner, for their active participation in national and international hackathons, and for their Google Developer Student Club (GDSC) chapter, which is one of the most active GDSCs in Uttar Pradesh.

The department maintains strong ties with leading tech companies including Google, Microsoft, Amazon, and various product startups, ensuring that students receive industry-oriented training, live project experience, and superior placement outcomes.

KIET Coding Warriors & Achievements

🏆 Coding Warriors — KIET's Competitive Programming Club

KIET Coding Warriors is the flagship competitive programming club of KIET Ghaziabad's CSE department. It regularly trains students for Codeforces, LeetCode, CodeChef, and ACM-ICPC contests. Key achievements include:

  • Multiple teams qualified for ACM-ICPC Asia Regionals
  • Consistent top-100 finishes in CodeChef Cook-Offs and Long Challenges
  • Winners of Smart India Hackathon (SIH) — Hardware and Software editions
  • Winners of HackFest KIET — annual inter-college hackathon attracting 500+ participants
  • Teams placed in Google Hash Code global rounds
  • Active participation in Microsoft Imagine Cup regional rounds

🌐 GDSC KIET — Google Developer Student Club

The GDSC chapter at KIET Ghaziabad is one of the most active in Uttar Pradesh. Led by student leaders mentored by Google, the club organizes weekly tech talks, hands-on workshops on Android, Firebase, TensorFlow, and Google Cloud, and hosts the annual Solution Challenge where teams build solutions for UN SDGs using Google technologies.

Laboratory Facilities

💻 Programming Lab

100+ workstations, Visual Studio Code, PyCharm, IntelliJ IDEA, GCC compilers for all major languages.

🤖 AI & ML Lab

GPU-enabled servers with TensorFlow, Keras, PyTorch; research projects and capstone work hosted here.

☁️ Google Cloud Lab

GCP credits for students via GDSC, hands-on cloud deployments, Kubernetes and Docker training.

🔐 Cyber Security Lab

Penetration testing tools, network analyzers, CTF practice environment; hosts inter-college CTFs.

📱 Android & Web Dev Lab

Android Studio, Flutter SDK, React/Node.js environment; mobile app and web development practicals.

🗄️ Database & Networks Lab

SQL Server, Oracle, NoSQL (MongoDB); Cisco routers and Packet Tracer for network simulation labs.
