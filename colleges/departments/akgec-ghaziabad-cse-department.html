Department Overview

The Computer Science & Engineering Department at Ajay Kumar Garg Engineering College (AKGEC), Ghaziabad has built a formidable reputation as one of the most industry-integrated CSE departments in the entire AKTU ecosystem. AKGEC's CSE department distinguishes itself through strategic industry collaborations that go far beyond conventional placement tie-ups.

The department holds an active IBM Academic Initiative partnership that brings IBM technologies, tools, and certifications directly into the curriculum. Students access IBM Cloud, IBM Watson AI tools, and IBM SkillsBuild platform as part of their regular coursework, graduating with both a B.Tech degree and globally recognized IBM credentials.

Additionally, AKGEC CSE is an AWS Academy institution, offering AWS-certified cloud computing coursework and enabling students to earn AWS Cloud Practitioner and AWS Solutions Architect Associate certifications during their degree programme. This dual-credential approach makes AKGEC CSE graduates uniquely competitive in the job market.

Perhaps most remarkably, AKGEC has recorded an international placement of 1.13 CPA (Crore Per Annum) — the highest international package achieved by any AKTU-affiliated private college student — cementing its place as a top placement-performing institution in UP.

IBM & AWS Collaboration Programmes

🔵 IBM Academic Initiative at AKGEC CSE

Under the IBM Academic Initiative, AKGEC CSE students gain access to:

  • IBM Cloud Pak for data science and AI workloads
  • IBM Watson Studio for ML model building and deployment
  • IBM SkillsBuild for digital credential pathways
  • Guest lectures by IBM certified professionals every semester
  • Internship pipelines to IBM India and IBM Global Services

🟠 AWS Academy — AKGEC CSE Cloud Lab

As an AWS Academy member institution, AKGEC provides:

  • Free AWS Educate credits for all CSE students
  • Faculty-led AWS Cloud Practitioner exam preparation
  • Lab access for EC2, S3, Lambda, RDS, and VPC configurations
  • Pathway to AWS Solutions Architect (Associate) certification

Lab Facilities

🔵 IBM Lab @ AKGEC

Dedicated IBM-branded lab with Watson Studio, IBM Cloud Pak, DB2 database; IBM-certified instructors lead workshops.

🟠 AWS Cloud Lab

AWS Educate sandbox environment, 50+ workstations with browser-based AWS console access; hands-on EC2, S3, Lambda labs.

🤖 ML & AI Research Lab

GPU servers (NVIDIA Tesla), TensorFlow, PyTorch; active research in computer vision and NLP.

🔐 Security & Networks Lab

Kali Linux, Metasploit, GNS3, Cisco hardware routers; ethical hacking workshops every semester.

💻 Programming & Development Lab

120+ workstations, multi-OS setup (Windows/Linux), all major IDEs; used for coding labs Sem 1–4.

📊 Data Science Lab

Jupyter Notebooks, R Studio, Tableau, Power BI; used for analytics, visualization, and capstone projects.
