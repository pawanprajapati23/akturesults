Department Overview

The Department of Computer Science & Engineering at IET Lucknow stands as one of the most sought-after CSE departments among all AKTU-affiliated government colleges in Uttar Pradesh. Established with the vision of producing globally competent software engineers and researchers, the department has consistently delivered outstanding academic results, stellar placement figures, and significant contributions to research in emerging technologies.

The department offers a rigorous B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering programme structured over eight semesters, covering foundational computing theory, systems programming, data structures, machine learning, cloud computing, and cybersecurity. Students benefit from both academic depth and industry-oriented training through tie-ups with leading technology firms.

IET Lucknow's CSE department is particularly noted for its highly qualified faculty, most of whom hold PhDs from IITs and NITs, its cutting-edge laboratory infrastructure, active coding culture via competitive programming clubs, and a dedicated GATE preparation cell that has helped hundreds of students secure admissions to IITs, NITs, and PSUs every year.

Laboratory Facilities

The CSE department at IET Lucknow houses well-equipped, industry-standard laboratories that complement theoretical learning with practical exposure.

🖥️ Programming & Data Structures Lab

90-node high-performance workstations with GCC, Python, Java IDEs; used for C, C++, Java, and DS practicals from Sem 1–3.

🤖 Artificial Intelligence & ML Lab

NVIDIA GPU servers, TensorFlow, PyTorch, Scikit-learn; supports ML, Deep Learning, NLP projects. Research collaborations active.

☁️ Cloud Computing Lab

AWS Educate partnership, Azure portal access, private OpenStack cluster. Students earn AWS Cloud Practitioner certifications.

🔐 Cybersecurity Lab

Kali Linux workstations, Metasploit, Wireshark, network traffic analysis tools. Hosts Capture-The-Flag competitions.

🗄️ Database & Networks Lab

Oracle 19c, MySQL, PostgreSQL servers; Cisco Packet Tracer and GNS3 for networking simulations.

📱 IoT & Embedded Systems Lab

Raspberry Pi, Arduino, ESP32 kits; sensors and actuators for IoT prototyping and smart systems development.

GATE Coaching & Higher Studies

📖 GATE Preparation Cell — IET Lucknow CSE

IET Lucknow operates a structured GATE Coaching Cell within the department, offering free weekend lectures, mock tests, and doubt-clearing sessions for CSE students. An average of 20–25 students per batch qualify GATE (CS/IT paper) and secure admissions to IITs (IIT Delhi, Bombay, Kharagpur), NITs (NIT Trichy, Surathkal, Allahabad), and PSUs like BSNL, NHPC, and MDL.

Higher Studies Options After IET Lucknow CSE

  • M.Tech (AKTU / IITs / NITs) — via GATE score
  • MS Abroad — USA (MSCS), Canada (MEng), Germany, Australia
  • MBA — CAT / GMAT post B.Tech for product/tech management roles
  • Research (PhD) — JRF/SRF via UGC-NET or direct PhD at IITs
  • PSU Jobs — BSNL, NHPC, MDL via GATE score
