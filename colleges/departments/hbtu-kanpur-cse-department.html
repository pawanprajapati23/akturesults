Department Overview

Harcourt Butler Technical University (HBTU), Kanpur is one of UP's most esteemed state technical universities, with a history spanning over a century. The CSE Department at HBTU Kanpur carries this legacy forward — producing technically exceptional graduates who regularly secure positions at the world's most competitive technology companies.

HBTU Kanpur's CSE department is distinguished by its research-focused faculty, most holding PhDs from IITs and prominent institutes, its sophisticated research laboratories in AI, HPC, and cybersecurity, and its extraordinary placement outcomes — including Google, Goldman Sachs Technology, and multiple product companies that have offered packages up to 65 LPA.

As an autonomous institution, HBTU CSE has the flexibility to innovate its curriculum beyond AKTU's standard prescriptions, integrating advanced electives in quantum computing, AI ethics, and fintech engineering. This academic agility, combined with a strong alumni network, creates a uniquely competitive CSE department among UP government universities.

Research Labs
