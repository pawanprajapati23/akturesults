🎁 Tuition Fee Waiver (TFW / FW) Seat Rules in UPTAC

What is AKTU Tuition Fee Waiver (FW)?

AKTU reserves 5% supernumerary seats in every branch across all affiliated government and private colleges under the Tuition Fee Waiver (TFW) scheme.

  • 100% Tuition Fee Exemption: Selected FW candidates do not pay the ₹1.2L - ₹1.4L tuition fee portion! They only pay exam & university registration charges (~₹10,000 to ₹15,000/yr).
  • Eligibility Criteria: Annual family income of parents must be less than ₹6.0 Lakhs per annum (Valid UP Domicile & Income Certificate required).
  • Selection Basis: Allotted purely on JEE Main merit rank during UPTAC choice filling. Select branch options with suffix (FW) during counselling.

📋 Mandatory Documents for Fee Refund & Admission Reporting

During UPTAC 2026 physical reporting at allotted colleges, bring original and 2 sets of photocopies of:

  • UPTAC Seat Allotment Letter & Fee Receipt (₹20,000 / ₹12,000)
  • JEE Main 2026 Rank Card & Admit Card
  • Class 10th & 12th Marksheet and Passing Certificates
  • Category / Sub-Category Certificate (OBC-NCL issued after April 1, 2026)
  • Income Certificate for EWS & Fee Waiver (TFW) claims
  • Transfer Certificate (TC) & Migration Certificate
  • Medical Fitness Certificate & Character Certificate
