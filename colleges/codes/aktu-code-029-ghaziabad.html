🏛️ About KIET Group Ghaziabad (Code: 029) - Campus Overview

Established in 1998, KIET Group Ghaziabad (College Code: 029) is a premier Autonomous / AKTU Affiliated engineering institution spread across a 18 Acres campus in Delhi-NCR, Ghaziabad. Accredited with NAAC A+ grade, the institute has an annual approved intake of 1440 seats across B.Tech, M.Tech, MBA, and MCA programmes.

🎯 Admission & Cutoff Procedure 2026

Admissions to B.Tech 1st Year at KIET Group Ghaziabad are strictly based on JEE Main 2026 AIR Rank through UPTAC (formerly UPSEE) Online Counselling. 85% of total seats are filled via UPTAC quota, while 15% seats are reserved for Management / Direct Merit quota as per AICTE and AKTU guidelines.
