📖 About TMU Moradabad

Teerthanker Mahaveer University (TMU), Moradabad is a NAAC-A accredited private university spread across 60 acres in Moradabad. Established in 2008 under Teerthanker Mahaveer University Act, TMU has grown into one of UP's premier private universities offering engineering, management, pharmacy, law, and medical programmes.
