📖 About ECB Unnao

Engineering College Bikru (ECB) is a government engineering college in Unnao established in 2010. Affiliated to AKTU, it offers B.Tech in 4 branches serving students from Unnao, Kanpur, and Lucknow districts.

Infrastructure: 10-acre Bikru campus with library, 10 labs, hostel blocks, and sports facilities.
