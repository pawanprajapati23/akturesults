📖 About JSS Noida

JSS Academy of Technical Education (JSSATEN), Noida is one of the most reputed AKTU-affiliated private engineering colleges in Delhi-NCR. Founded in 1998 under JSS Mahavidyapeetha, Mysuru. Known for a highest placement of 57 LPA (Palo Alto Networks) and strong NAAC-A accreditation.

Infrastructure: 28-acre Sector 62 Noida campus with central library (90,000+ volumes), 12 specialised labs, Cisco Networking Lab, Data Science Centre, Cloud Computing Lab, 1000-seat seminar hall, boys and girls hostel blocks, gymnasium, and round-the-clock security.
