📖 About PSIT Kanpur

PSIT Kanpur is the top AKTU-affiliated autonomous engineering college in the Kanpur region. NAAC-A accredited, spread across 42 acres on NH-25. Oracle University Centre and IBM SkillsBuild Centre on campus. 40 LPA highest placement. TCS, Infosys, Wipro are anchor recruiters with 88% placement rate.

Infrastructure: 42-acre campus with 6 AC academic buildings, 26 advanced labs, Oracle University Centre, IBM SkillsBuild Centre, 4 hostel blocks (1600+ beds), gymnasium, cricket ground, basketball courts, food court, health centre, and 1400-seat convention hall.
