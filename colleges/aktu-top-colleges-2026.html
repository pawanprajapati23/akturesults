Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about AKTU colleges and admissions.

Among government colleges, IET Lucknow and HBTI Kanpur are consistently regarded as the top AKTU colleges. For private colleges, KIET Ghaziabad ranks highest based on placements, infrastructure and academic reputation. KNIT Sultanpur and BIET Jhansi are also top government choices.
For IET Lucknow and HBTI Kanpur CSE/IT, you typically need a UPSEE rank under 3,000. For KIET and JSS Noida, ranks under 8,000 work for CSE. Other branches and Tier 2/3 colleges accept ranks up to 30,000+. Government colleges are more competitive due to limited seats.
Government colleges (IET, HBTI, KNIT, BIET, RECs) offer excellent education at ₹60,000–80,000 for the full BTech. They often have better faculty-student culture. Top private colleges (KIET, JSS, AKGEC) have better infrastructure and industry connections but cost ₹4–8 lakhs. If you get a government seat — prefer it.
AKTU has over 800 affiliated colleges across Uttar Pradesh offering engineering, management, pharmacy and architecture programs. Quality varies significantly — this list covers the top 40 to help you focus your research.
Top-tier AKTU colleges (Tier 1 & 2) have strong placement records with average packages of ₹5–8 LPA and highest packages of ₹30–54 LPA at premier institutes. Placement rates vary from 75–92% at top colleges. Tier 3 colleges average ₹3–4.5 LPA.
Yes, college change is possible through subsequent UPTAC counselling rounds if seats are available. An internal college-change process also exists subject to university rules. Check the official AKTU/UPTAC portal for current-year guidelines.
