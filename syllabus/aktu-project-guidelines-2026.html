How to Download AKTU Syllabus

  • Step 1: Visit official AKTU website (aktu.ac.in)
  • Step 2: Go to 'Academics' or 'Examination' section
  • Step 3: Select 'Syllabus' from the menu
  • Step 4: Choose your program (B.Tech, MBA, MCA etc.)
  • Step 5: Select semester and branch
  • Step 6: Click on download link to get PDF
  • Step 7: Save the syllabus for offline reference
💡 Study Tip: Go through the complete syllabus at the start of semester. This helps in planning your study schedule and understanding exam pattern.

Importance of Following Syllabus

  • Exam Preparation: Questions are asked from prescribed syllabus only
  • Time Management: Know exactly what to study and in how much depth
  • Resource Planning: Get recommended books and reference materials
  • Unit-wise Study: Divide syllabus into manageable units for study
  • Weightage Understanding: Know important topics with higher weightage

Credit System Explained

  • Each subject has assigned credits based on contact hours
  • Theory subjects typically have 3-4 credits
  • Practical/Lab subjects have 1-2 credits
  • Total credits per semester: 20-24 credits
  • Credits are used for SGPA and CGPA calculation
  • Minimum credits required for degree completion

Frequently Asked Questions

Where to download official AKTU syllabus?

Visit aktu.ac.in and navigate to Academics > Syllabus section. Select your program and semester to download PDF.

Is syllabus same for all AKTU colleges?

Yes, all affiliated colleges under AKTU follow the same syllabus for each program and semester.

Does AKTU change syllabus every year?

No, syllabus remains mostly same. Minor revisions may happen once in 3-4 years. Always check for latest syllabus on official website.

Can I get subject-wise syllabus separately?

Yes, AKTU provides both complete semester syllabus and individual subject syllabi on the official portal.
