About AKTU Attendance Calculator

This free attendance calculator helps AKTU students track their attendance percentage and determine how many classes they can skip or need to attend to maintain the required 75% attendance.

AKTU Attendance Requirements

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU) requires students to maintain a minimum of 75% attendance to be eligible for semester examinations. Failure to meet this requirement can result in:

  • Debarment from semester exams
  • Need to repeat the semester
  • Academic penalties
  • Impact on final grades

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter the total number of classes held so far
  2. Enter the number of classes you have attended
  3. Click "Calculate Attendance" to see your results
  4. Check your current percentage and required classes

💡 Pro Tips

  • ✓ Regularly track your attendance to avoid last-minute surprises
  • ✓ Aim for 80%+ attendance for buffer in emergencies
  • ✓ Medical leave may be considered with proper documentation
  • ✓ Contact your college for specific attendance policies

Understanding the Results

Current Percentage: Your actual attendance percentage based on classes attended.

Classes Shortage: How many classes short you are from 75% requirement (if applicable).

Can Skip: How many classes you can afford to miss while staying above 75%.

Need to Attend: How many consecutive classes you must attend to reach 75%.

Consequences of Low Attendance

Students with less than 75% attendance may face:

  • Examination Debarment: Not allowed to appear in semester exams
  • Fee Penalty: Additional fees for repeating the semester
  • Academic Delay: Delay in graduation
  • Placement Impact: Lower attendance affects placement eligibility

Medical & Special Cases

AKTU may grant attendance relaxation in genuine cases such as:

  • Medical emergencies with valid certificates
  • Participation in university-approved events
  • Family emergencies (with proper documentation)
  • Sports competitions representing university

Always contact your college administration for such cases.
