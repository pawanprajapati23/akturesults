🔄 AKTU Revaluation Process 2026

Complete Guide for Rechecking & Revaluation Application

What is AKTU Revaluation?

AKTU revaluation is a process where students can apply for rechecking of their answer sheets if they are not satisfied with their exam results. The university re-evaluates the answer sheet and declares the updated result if there's any change in marks.

⚠️ Important Notice

Revaluation applications are accepted only within the specified time period after result declaration. Make sure to apply before the last date.

📋 Important Information

Detail Information
Revaluation Fee ₹400 per subject
Application Mode Online (ERP Portal)
Time Period Within 15 days of result declaration
Result Declaration 30-45 days after application
Payment Mode Online (Debit/Credit Card, Net Banking)

📝 Step-by-Step Application Process

1 Login to ERP Portal

Visit the AKTU ERP portal at erp.aktu.ac.in and login with your credentials (Enrollment number and password).

2 Navigate to Revaluation Section

After logging in, go to Examination → Apply for Revaluation section in the menu.

3 Select Subjects

Select the subject(s) for which you want to apply for revaluation. You can select multiple subjects.

4 Pay Revaluation Fee

Pay the revaluation fee (₹400 per subject) through online payment gateway. Keep the payment receipt safe.

5 Submit Application

Review your application details and click submit. Take a printout of the application form for your records.

6 Track Application Status

You can track your revaluation application status through the ERP portal under revaluation status section.

✅ Documents Required

  • Valid enrollment number and password
  • Original marksheet (for reference)
  • Payment details (Debit/Credit card or Net Banking)
  • Valid email ID and mobile number

💡 Important Points to Remember

  • Revaluation can be applied for theory papers only, not for practical exams
  • Marks can increase, decrease, or remain the same after revaluation
  • Application must be submitted within the deadline (usually 15 days)
  • Fee once paid is non-refundable
  • Only answer sheet rechecking is done, no copying or re-totaling
  • Keep your payment receipt and application form safe
  • Results are usually declared within 30-45 days

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: Can I apply for revaluation for all subjects?

Yes, you can apply for revaluation for one or more subjects, but you need to pay ₹400 per subject.

Q2: What is the last date for revaluation application?

The last date is usually 15 days from the result declaration date. Check the official AKTU notification for exact dates.

Q3: Can my marks decrease after revaluation?

Yes, marks can increase, decrease, or remain the same after revaluation. However, AKTU considers the higher marks in most cases.

Q4: How can I check my revaluation result?

Revaluation results are available on the ERP portal under the revaluation result section. You'll also receive an SMS/email notification.

Q5: Is revaluation fee refundable?

No, the revaluation fee is non-refundable even if your marks don't change.

📞 Need Help?

For any queries regarding revaluation process, contact AKTU examination cell or visit the official AKTU website for latest updates.
