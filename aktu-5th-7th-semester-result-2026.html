What to Do When AKTU Result Server is Not Working

It's very common for the AKTU result portal to face heavy traffic immediately after result declaration, causing server slowdowns or temporary unavailability. Don't panic - this is normal and happens every year. Here are proven solutions to access your result when the server is not responding:

Immediate Solutions

  • Try Different Times: The server is usually most crowded between 10 AM to 6 PM. Try checking your result early morning (6-8 AM) or late night (11 PM onwards) when traffic is lower.
  • Use Multiple Browsers: If Chrome isn't working, try Firefox, Edge, or Opera. Sometimes browser-specific caching issues cause problems. You can also try incognito/private mode.
  • Clear Browser Cache: Press Ctrl+Shift+Delete (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+Delete (Mac), select "Cached images and files" and clear it. Then try accessing the result portal again.
  • Switch Networks: If you're using WiFi, switch to mobile data or vice versa. Sometimes specific ISPs face routing issues to AKTU servers.
  • Disable VPN: If you're using any VPN service, disable it. VPNs can sometimes cause connectivity issues with educational portals.
  • Try Alternative Links: AKTU often activates multiple mirror servers during result season. Check the official website for alternative links or backup portals.

Technical Troubleshooting

  • Flush DNS Cache: Open Command Prompt (Windows) and type: ipconfig /flushdns or on Mac/Linux: sudo dscacheutil -flushcache
  • Check Server Status: Visit downdetector.com or isitdownrightnow.com and search for "AKTU" to see if others are also facing issues.
  • Disable Extensions: Browser extensions, especially ad-blockers, can interfere with portal functionality. Try disabling them temporarily.
  • Update Browser: Make sure you're using the latest version of your web browser for optimal compatibility.
⚠️ Important Note: Never use third-party websites claiming to show AKTU results. Only use official AKTU websites (aktu.ac.in and erp.aktu.ac.in). Third-party sites may show incorrect results or steal your personal information.

Understanding Your AKTU Result - Marks, Grades & SGPA Calculation

Your AKTU result contains several important components that determine your academic standing. Let's understand each element in detail:

Components of AKTU Marksheet

  • Internal Marks: These are marks awarded by your college for attendance, assignments, presentations, and internal tests. Maximum internal marks are usually 25-30 depending on the subject.
  • External Marks: Marks obtained in the university examination (written exam). These carry higher weightage, typically 70-75 marks per subject.
  • Total Marks: Sum of internal + external marks for each subject.
  • Grade Points: Your marks are converted to a 10-point scale:
    • O (Outstanding): 90-100 marks = 10 points
    • A+ (Excellent): 80-89 marks = 9 points
    • A (Very Good): 70-79 marks = 8 points
    • B+ (Good): 60-69 marks = 7 points
    • B (Above Average): 50-59 marks = 6 points
    • C (Average): 40-49 marks = 5 points
    • P (Pass): 35-39 marks = 4 points
    • F (Fail): Below 35 marks = 0 points
  • SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average): Average of all grade points obtained in that particular semester. Formula: (Sum of Grade Points × Credits) / Total Credits
  • CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average): Overall average of all semesters completed till date. This is what appears on your final degree.

Pass Criteria for AKTU Examinations

To pass a subject in AKTU, you must fulfill these conditions:

  • Minimum 40% in theory paper (combined internal + external)
  • Minimum 50% in practical/sessional subjects
  • Overall pass percentage of 40% considering all subjects
  • No minimum marks criterion for practicals separately in most programs
  • SGPA should be 4.0 or above to be considered as pass
📊 CGPA to Percentage Conversion: To convert your CGPA to percentage, multiply it by 10. For example, if your CGPA is 7.5, your percentage is 75%. However, official conversion may vary, so always use the university's conversion formula when required for placements or higher education.

What If You Have Backlogs? - AKTU Back Paper Information

If you have failed in one or more subjects (called "backlogs" or "back papers" in AKTU terminology), don't worry - you can clear them in the upcoming carry over examinations. Here's what you need to know:

Back Paper Examination Process

  • Eligibility: Students who have failed in up to 4 subjects can appear for carry over exams while continuing with the next semester.
  • Registration: You need to fill the back paper examination form (usually online through college or ERP portal) within the specified dates.
  • Examination Fee: Separate fees are charged per subject for back paper exams (approximately ₹300-500 per subject).
  • Exam Schedule: AKTU conducts carry over examinations twice a year - usually in May-June and November-December.
  • Result Declaration: Back paper results are typically declared 30-45 days after the exam completion.
  • No Attempt Limit: You can attempt to clear a backlog subject multiple times until you pass (no maximum attempt restriction in most programs).

Impact of Backlogs

  • Most companies allow students with 1-2 backlogs to sit for campus placements
  • Your final degree will only be awarded after clearing all subjects
  • Backlogs don't appear on the final degree marksheet, only on semester-wise marksheets
  • For higher education (M.Tech, MBA), most institutes accept students with cleared backlogs

AKTU Revaluation Process 2026

If you're not satisfied with your marks in any subject, AKTU provides the option to apply for revaluation (also called rechecking or scrutiny). Here's everything you need to know:

What is Revaluation?

Revaluation is a process where your answer sheet is checked again by a different examiner. The university verifies that all questions have been evaluated and marks have been correctly totaled. However, the actual content evaluation may not change significantly.

How to Apply for Revaluation

  1. Check Eligibility You can apply for revaluation only for theory papers, not for practical or internal marks.
  2. Apply Within Deadline Revaluation application window is usually open for 7-10 days after result declaration. Don't miss the deadline!
  3. Fill Online Application Login to AKTU ERP portal, go to Examination section, and select "Apply for Revaluation" option.
  4. Select Subjects Choose the subjects you want to get revaluated. You can select one or multiple subjects.
  5. Pay Revaluation Fee Pay the required fee online (approximately ₹500-1000 per subject) through credit card, debit card, or net banking.
  6. Submit and Save Receipt After successful payment, download and save the application receipt for future reference.
  7. Wait for Results Revaluation results are usually declared within 30-45 days of application submission.

Important Points About Revaluation

  • Marks can increase, decrease, or remain the same after revaluation
  • If marks increase, updated marksheet will be issued
  • Revaluation fee is non-refundable, even if marks don't change
  • You cannot view your answer sheet during revaluation process
  • Second revaluation is not permitted in most cases
  • Apply only if you're confident about significant marking error
💰 Cost-Benefit Analysis: Before applying for revaluation, consider if the potential mark increase is worth the fee. If you're just 2-3 marks away from the next grade or passing, it might be worth trying. Otherwise, it's better to prepare well for carry over exam.
