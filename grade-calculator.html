About AKTU CGPA Calculator

This free AKTU CGPA Calculator helps students calculate their Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA) and Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) according to the AKTU grading system.

AKTU Grading System

Grade Marks Range Grade Points Performance
O 90-100 10 Outstanding
A+ 80-89 9 Excellent
A 70-79 8 Very Good
B+ 60-69 7 Good
B 50-59 6 Above Average
C 40-49 5 Average
P 35-39 4 Pass
F Below 35 0 Fail

How to Calculate SGPA

Formula: SGPA = (Sum of Credit × Grade Point) / Total Credits

Example: If you have 5 subjects with different credits and grades, multiply each credit by its grade point, add them all, and divide by total credits.

How to Calculate CGPA

Formula: CGPA = (Sum of all SGPAs) / Number of Semesters

Add all your semester SGPAs and divide by the number of semesters completed.

Why Calculate CGPA?

  • Track your academic progress across semesters
  • Required for placements and higher education applications
  • Helps identify areas for improvement
  • Essential for scholarship eligibility
