How to check AKTU result 2026? ▼

Method 1: Login to erp.aktu.ac.in with enrollment number and password.
Method 2: Use One View option (no login required).
Method 3: Check our results page for direct links.

When will AKTU result 2026 be declared? ▼

AKTU usually declares results within 30-45 days after exams. Check our website regularly for updates and notifications.

How to calculate CGPA in AKTU? ▼

Formula: CGPA = (Sum of all semester SGPA) / Number of semesters
Example: If Sem 1 = 8.5, Sem 2 = 8.0, Sem 3 = 8.2
CGPA = (8.5 + 8.0 + 8.2) / 3 = 8.23

AKTU result server down - what to do? ▼

1. Wait for 15-30 minutes and try again

3. Clear browser cache and cookies

🔐 ERP Login Questions

What is AKTU ERP login password? ▼

Default Password: Your date of birth in DDMMYYYY format
Example: If DOB is 15th January 2005, password is 15012005
Important: Change your password after first login for security.
Check our complete ERP login guide.

ERP login showing "Invalid credentials" - why? ▼

1. Double-check enrollment number (no spaces)
2. Ensure password is in DDMMYYYY format
3. Check if caps lock is on
4. Try default password (date of birth)
5. Contact your college if problem persists

Forgot ERP password - how to reset? ▼

1. Click "Forgot Password" on login page
2. Enter enrollment number and registered email
3. Follow instructions sent to your email
4. If not working, contact college IT department
5. They can reset your password

How to apply for AKTU revaluation? ▼

1. Login to ERP within 15 days of result
2. Go to Examination → Revaluation
3. Select subjects
4. Pay ₹400 per subject
5. Submit application

🎓 General Questions

How many back papers are allowed in AKTU? ▼

You can carry forward backlogs, but must clear ALL backlogs before final year completion to receive your degree. Check with your college for specific limits per semester.

Can marks decrease in revaluation? ▼

Technically yes, marks can increase, decrease, or remain same. However, AKTU usually considers the higher marks between original and revaluation results.

Is AKTU degree valid for government jobs? ▼

Yes! AKTU is a state government university and its degrees are valid for all government jobs, higher education, and abroad studies. It's UGC approved and NAAC accredited.
