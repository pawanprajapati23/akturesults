AKTU Branch-Wise Placement & Career Roadmap 2026

Comprehensive guide to salary packages, off-campus placement drives, and high-income skills for AKTU engineering and management graduates.

🚀 Branch-Wise Salary & Placement Breakdown

💻 B.Tech Computer Science (CSE/IT)

Average Salary: ₹6.5 LPA - ₹9.5 LPA

Highest Off-Campus Package: ₹45 LPA - ₹1.13 Crore (Amazon, Microsoft, Google)

Top Skills Required: Data Structures & Algorithms (LeetCode 300+ solved), MERN Stack / Java Spring Boot, System Design.

⚡ B.Tech Electronics & Comm (ECE/EEE)

Average Salary: ₹5.5 LPA - ₹7.5 LPA

Top Core Recruiters: Intel, Qualcomm, Texas Instruments, Schneider Electric, TCS Ninja.

Top Skills Required: Verilog, Embedded Systems, VLSI Design, Python/C++ Programming.

⚙️ B.Tech Mechanical & Civil (ME/CE)

Average Salary: ₹4.5 LPA - ₹6.5 LPA

Top Options: GATE Exam (PSUs like BHEL, NTPC, IOCL), L&T, Tata Motors, IT Transition.

Top Skills Required: AutoCAD, SolidWorks, REVIT, Primavera, Core Gate Preparation.
