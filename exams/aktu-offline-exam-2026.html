Common FAQs

What if I lost my admit card?

You can download your admit card again from the ERP portal using your login credentials or enrollment number and date of birth.

Can I appear in exam without admit card?

No, admit card is mandatory to appear in AKTU examinations. Without admit card, you will not be allowed to enter the examination hall.

What is the minimum attendance required?

Generally, 75% attendance is required to be eligible for AKTU examinations. However, check with your college for exact requirements.

When will exam results be declared?

AKTU typically declares results within 30-45 days after completion of examinations. Keep checking the official portal for updates.
