AKTU Admit Card 2026: Direct ERP Hall Ticket Download Portal

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU) releases official semester exam admit cards (hall tickets) online on the AKTU ERP Student Portal (`erp.aktu.ac.in`) for B.Tech, MBA, MCA, B.Pharm, B.Arch, and Diploma candidates.

📋 How to Download AKTU Admit Card 2026 via ERP Login

  1. Visit official AKTU ERP portal: https://erp.aktu.ac.in.
  2. Enter your User ID (AKTU University Roll Number) and ERP Password.
  3. Complete the reCAPTCHA verification and click Log In.
  4. Navigate to the Admit Card / Examination Form Dashboard.
  5. Click on Print Hall Ticket / Download Admit Card 2026.
  6. Download the PDF and take a clear printout (preferably color print) on A4 size paper.

🚨 AKTU 75% Attendance Debarred List & Admit Card Hold Rules

Under official AKTU exam regulations, students with less than 75% attendance on the ERP portal are placed under the Debarred Category. If your admit card status shows "Admit Card Withheld / Debarred by Institute", contact your College Registrar office immediately to verify sessional attendance upload.
