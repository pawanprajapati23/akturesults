📋 How to Check AKTU Result - Complete Guide

Method 1: Using AKTU One View Portal (Recommended)

This is the fastest and easiest way to check your AKTU result without any login credentials. Perfect for quick result checking on result declaration day.

  1. Visit the Portal: Click on "AKTU One View Portal" button above or go to erp.aktu.ac.in/WebPages/OneView/OneView.aspx
  2. Select Examination: Choose your exam type (Regular/Backlog/Carry Over)
  3. Enter Roll Number: Type your university roll number carefully (check your admit card)
  4. Complete Verification: Enter the captcha or security code shown on screen
  5. Click Show Result: Press the button and your result will appear instantly
  6. View Details: Check your subject-wise marks, total marks, SGPA, and overall result status
  7. Download/Print: Take a screenshot or print the result for your records
✅ Advantages: No login needed • Works on mobile • Faster than ERP • Available immediately when results are declared • No password issues

Method 2: Using AKTU ERP Portal (Detailed Access)

The ERP portal provides comprehensive access to your complete academic records, including all previous semesters, attendance, internal marks, and downloadable marksheets.

  1. Open ERP Portal: Visit erp.aktu.ac.in
  2. Enter Username: Use your enrollment number or college roll number
  3. Enter Password: Type your ERP password (contact college if forgotten)
  4. Solve Captcha: Complete the security verification
  5. Click Login: Access your student dashboard
  6. Go to Examination: Navigate to Examination → View Result from menu
  7. Select Semester: Choose the semester for which you want to check result
  8. View Complete Details: See detailed marks, grace marks, grades, and CGPA
  9. Download Marksheet: Get digital copy of your marksheet in PDF format
💡 Pro Tip: Save your ERP login credentials securely. You'll need them throughout your academic journey for checking results, attendance, applying for exam forms, and downloading certificates.

⚠️ Important Information About AKTU Results

Understanding Your Result

Your AKTU result contains several important components that you need to understand:

  • Subject-wise Marks: Each subject shows internal marks (out of 30) and external marks (out of 70), totaling 100 marks per subject
  • Grade Points: Based on your total marks, you get grade points ranging from 0 (Fail) to 10 (Outstanding)
  • SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average): Your performance in current semester calculated using credit-weighted grade points
  • CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average): Overall performance across all completed semesters
  • Result Status: Pass/Fail/Compartment/Detained status clearly mentioned

Passing Criteria

To pass any subject in AKTU, you need to meet these minimum requirements:

  • Minimum 28 marks out of 70 in theory (external) exam
  • Minimum 12 marks out of 30 in internal assessment
  • Overall minimum 40 marks out of 100 (35 for some subjects)
  • Minimum 75% attendance in each subject (or medical certificate)
⚠️ Important: Even if you score well in internal marks, failing to secure minimum external marks will result in failure. Both components are mandatory for passing.

What to Do After Checking Result?

If You Passed:

  • Download and save your digital marksheet from ERP portal
  • Calculate your CGPA using our CGPA Calculator
  • Check for any discrepancies in marks immediately
  • Prepare for next semester or placements

If You Have Backlogs:

  • Note down the subjects in which you failed
  • Check the carry-over exam schedule (usually within 2-3 months)
  • Start preparation immediately - don't wait for exam notification
  • Read our guide: How to Clear AKTU Backlogs
  • Apply for carry-over exam form when announced

If You Think There's an Error:

  • Verify your roll number and semester carefully
  • Cross-check with your answer booklet and admit card
  • Apply for revaluation within the specified deadline (usually 15 days)
  • Contact your college examination cell for guidance
  • Keep fees ready for revaluation (around ₹200-300 per subject)

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: When will AKTU Result 2026 be declared?

AKTU typically declares results within 45-60 days after examination completion. Odd semester results (1st, 3rd, 5th, 7th sem) usually come in February-March, while even semester results (2nd, 4th, 6th, 8th sem) are declared in July-August. However, exact dates vary each year and are announced officially by the university.

Q2: What if AKTU result portal is not working or server is down?

On result declaration day, AKTU servers often face heavy traffic causing slowdowns or temporary crashes. If you face issues: (1) Try after 30 minutes when traffic reduces, (2) Use incognito/private mode in browser, (3) Clear browser cache and cookies, (4) Try different browser or device, (5) Check early morning or late night when traffic is low. Results remain accessible for months, so don't panic if you can't check immediately.

Q3: Can I check AKTU result without roll number?

No, your university roll number is mandatory to check AKTU result. The roll number is unique to each student and semester. You can find it on your admit card, student ID card, or college records. If you've lost your admit card, contact your college examination cell to get your roll number.

Q4: How do I download my AKTU marksheet?

Digital marksheet can be downloaded from the ERP portal after result declaration. Login to ERP → Go to Examination → Download Marksheet → Select semester → Download PDF. However, original marksheet is issued by your college after 2-3 months and requires university verification. Digital marksheet is valid for most purposes including job applications.

Q5: What is the difference between AKTU One View and ERP portal?

One View portal is for quick result checking without login - you just need roll number. It's faster and works well on result day. ERP portal requires username/password but provides complete academic details including attendance, previous results, internal marks, fee status, and downloadable marksheets. Use One View for quick checking, use ERP for detailed records.

Q6: I forgot my AKTU ERP password. What should I do?

If you forgot your ERP password: (1) Use the "Forgot Password" option on login page and follow instructions, (2) Contact your college examination cell or admin department - they can reset it, (3) You can still check result using One View portal without password. For future, save your credentials securely or use password manager.

Q7: How is CGPA calculated in AKTU?

CGPA is the weighted average of SGPA from all semesters. Formula: Sum of (SGPA × Credits) for all semesters ÷ Total credits. For example, if you have SGPA 8, 7.5, 8.2, 8.5 in 4 semesters with 20 credits each, CGPA = (8×20 + 7.5×20 + 8.2×20 + 8.5×20) ÷ 80 = 8.05. Use our CGPA Calculator for instant calculation.

Q8: Can I apply for revaluation if I'm not satisfied with my marks?

Yes, AKTU allows revaluation (rechecking) of answer sheets. You need to apply online through ERP portal within 15 days of result declaration. Revaluation fee is approximately ₹200-300 per subject. However, only totaling of marks and answer checking is done - no re-evaluation of answers. Revaluation results come within 1-2 months. Apply only if you genuinely feel there's an error.
