How to Check CBSE Result 2026 Online

Follow these simple steps to check your CBSE Class 10th or 12th result online:

  1. Visit Official Website
    Go to cbse.gov.in or cbseresults.nic.in
  2. Click on Result Link
    Look for "CBSE Class 10th Result 2026" or "CBSE Class 12th Result 2026" link on homepage
  3. Enter Your Details
    Enter your Roll Number, School Number, Centre Number, and Admit Card ID
  4. Submit Information
    Click on "Submit" button to view your result
  5. View & Download Result
    Your result will be displayed on screen. Download and take printout for future reference
💡 Pro Tip: Keep your Admit Card handy as you'll need the Roll Number and School Code to check results.

Download CBSE Marksheet via DigiLocker

CBSE has partnered with DigiLocker to provide digital academic documents. Here's how to download:

  1. Visit digilocker.gov.in and login
  2. Click on "Education" section
  3. Select "Central Board of Secondary Education"
  4. Choose your class (10th or 12th)
  5. Enter your Roll Number and other required details
  6. Your digital marksheet will be available for download

CBSE Revaluation & Rechecking 2026

If you're not satisfied with your results, CBSE provides options for verification:

Verification of Marks

  • Apply within 21 days of result declaration
  • Fee: ₹500 per subject
  • Only totaling of marks is rechecked
  • No re-evaluation of answers

Photocopy of Answer Sheet

  • Available for both Class 10 and 12
  • Fee: ₹700 per subject
  • Apply through official CBSE portal

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. When will CBSE 10th and 12th results 2026 be declared?

CBSE typically declares Class 10th and 12th results in May. The exact date will be announced on the official website.

Q2. What if I forgot my Roll Number?

You can find your roll number on your admit card. Alternatively, contact your school to retrieve it.

Q3. Is compartment exam available?

Yes, students who fail in one or two subjects can appear for compartment exams, usually held in July-August.

Q4. How to calculate CGPA?

Add all grade points and divide by the number of subjects. The result is your CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average).

Q5. Can I get my result via mobile app?

Yes, CBSE has official mobile apps and you can also check results on DigiLocker app.
