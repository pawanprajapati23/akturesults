UP Board Latest Result Overview

UP Board Marking System

UP Board follows traditional percentage-based marking system:

Division Percentage Range
First Division 60% and above
Second Division 45% to 59.99%
Third Division 33% to 44.99%
Fail Below 33%

Important Documents After Result

  • Digital Marksheet: Available on official website immediately after result
  • Original Marksheet: Collect from your school within 15-30 days
  • Migration Certificate: Required for admission in other states
  • Character Certificate: Issued by school
  • Transfer Certificate: Needed for college admission

What if Result Shows "FAIL" or Compartment?

Compartment Exam:

  • Students failing in one or two subjects can appear for compartment exam
  • Compartment exams usually held in June-July
  • Apply through school within specified dates
  • Pay examination fee as per board norms

Re-appear Next Year:

  • If failed in more than two subjects, students need to re-appear next year
  • Can appear as regular or private candidate

UP Board Rechecking/Revaluation 2026

UP Board provides facility for marks rechecking:

  • Apply within: 7-10 days of result declaration
  • Fee: Around ₹100-200 per subject (may vary)
  • Process: Only addition of marks is rechecked, not answer evaluation
  • Apply through: School or online portal
  • Result: Updated result declared within 15-20 days

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. When will UP Board results 2026 be declared?

UP Board typically declares results in April. The exact date will be announced on upmsp.edu.in.

Q2. Can I check UP Board result by name?

No, results can only be checked using Roll Number. Name-wise search is not available.

Q3. Is there grace marking in UP Board?

Yes, UP Board provides grace marks up to 5% in each subject to help students pass.

Q4. How to download original marksheet?

Original marksheets are distributed through schools. You can also apply online on UP Board website for duplicate marksheet if lost.

Q5. What is passing marks in UP Board?

Minimum 33% marks in each subject and overall are required to pass.
