Maharashtra Board Result 2026 Overview

Maharashtra State Board of Secondary and Higher Secondary Education (MSBSHSE) conducts SSC (Class 10) and HSC (Class 12) examinations for students in Maharashtra. It's one of the largest state boards in India.

Exam Details Information
Board Name Maharashtra State Board (MSBSHSE)
SSC (10th) Result Expected in June 2026
HSC (12th) Result Expected in May 2026
Official Website mahahsscboard.in
Result Portals mahresult.nic.in, maharashtraeducation.com

How to Check Maharashtra Board Result 2026

  1. Visit Official Website
    Go to mahahsscboard.in or mahresult.nic.in
  2. Click Result Link
    Select "SSC Result 2026" or "HSC Result 2026"
  3. Enter Roll Number
    Enter your Roll Number and Mother's Name
  4. Submit & View
    Click submit to view your detailed result
  5. Download Marksheet
    Save PDF for future reference

Maharashtra Board Result via SMS

For SSC (Class 10th):

Format: MSSSSC[Seat Number]

Example: MSSSSC1234567

Send to: 57766

For HSC (Class 12th):

Format: MSHSC[Seat Number]

Example: MSHSC1234567

Send to: 57766

Maharashtra Board Grading System

Grade Marks Range Performance
A+ 80-100 Outstanding
A 60-79 Excellent
B 50-59 Very Good
C 40-49 Good
D 35-39 Pass
E Below 35 Fail

Note: Minimum 35% required in each subject to pass.

After Result - Important Steps

  • Verify all details on marksheet carefully
  • Collect original marksheet from school
  • Apply for college admissions immediately
  • Keep multiple photocopies of marksheet
  • Check scholarship eligibility

Revaluation/Verification Process

  • Apply within: 5-7 days after result declaration
  • Fee: Around ₹300-500 per subject
  • Online application: Through official portal
  • Photocopy of answer sheet: Available on request
  • Updated result: Within 30 days

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. When will Maharashtra Board results be declared?

HSC results typically in May and SSC results in June. Check official website for exact dates.

Q2. What is the passing marks?

Students need minimum 35% marks in each subject to pass.

Q3. Can I check result by name?

No, you need seat/roll number and mother's name to check result.

Q4. Is grace marking available?

Yes, grace marks up to 5 marks may be awarded to help students pass.

Q5. How to download digital marksheet?

Digital marksheet is available on official website immediately after result declaration.
