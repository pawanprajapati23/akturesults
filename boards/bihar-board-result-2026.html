Bihar Board Result 2026 Overview

Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB) conducts Matric (Class 10th) and Intermediate (Class 12th) examinations for lakhs of students across Bihar. The board is known for declaring results quickly, usually within 6-8 weeks of exam completion.

How to Check Bihar Board Result 2026

  1. Visit Official Website
    Go to biharboardonline.bihar.gov.in
  2. Select Your Exam
    Click on "Matric Result 2026" or "Inter Result 2026"
  3. Enter Details
    Enter Roll Code, Roll Number, and Security Code
  4. Submit & View Result
    Click "View Result" to see your marks
  5. Download Marksheet
    Save or print for future reference

Important After Result

  • Original marksheet distribution starts 15-20 days after result
  • Collect from your respective schools
  • Migration certificate for admission in other states
  • Compartment exam for students failing in 1-2 subjects
  • Apply for scholarship programs if eligible

Bihar Board Scrutiny 2026

If you're not satisfied with your marks, you can apply for scrutiny:

  • Apply within: 7-10 days of result declaration
  • Fee: ₹70 per subject for scrutiny
  • Process: Only marks totaling is rechecked
  • Apply through: Online portal or school
  • Updated result: Within 15-20 days

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. When will Bihar Board results 2026 be declared?

Bihar Board typically declares results in March. Official dates will be announced on the website.

Q2. What is the pass percentage in Bihar Board?

Students need minimum 33% marks in each subject and overall to pass.

Q3. Can I get my result via mobile app?

Yes, Bihar Board has official mobile apps for Android and iOS to check results.

Q4. What is compartment exam?

Students failing in one or two subjects can appear for compartment exam, usually held 2-3 months after main result.

Q5. How to get duplicate marksheet?

Apply online on Bihar Board website or visit board office with required documents and fee.
