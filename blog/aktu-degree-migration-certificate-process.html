AKTU Degree & Migration Certificate Guide

📅 Last Updated: July 2026 | 📚 Category: AKTU Rules & Regulations

When will I get my AKTU Final Degree?

AKTU officially awards degrees during its annual convocation (usually held between November and January). After the convocation, the degrees are dispatched directly to the student's registered address via India Post (Speed Post).

How to Download Provisional Degree Certificate (PDC)?

Since the original degree takes time, passed-out students can download their Provisional Degree Certificate (PDC) directly from the AKTU ERP portal.

  1. Login to AKTU ERP.
  2. Go to the My Profile section.
  3. Scroll down to find the Provisional Degree Certificate download link. (Ensure all your dues and backlogs are cleared).

How to apply for a Migration Certificate?

If you are planning to pursue higher studies in another university, you will need a Migration Certificate. You can apply for this online through the AKTU ERP student portal by paying a nominal fee. The certificate is then delivered to your address via post.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does AKTU send the original degree to the home address?
Yes, after the convocation, AKTU dispatches the original degrees to the student's address registered on the ERP portal via Speed Post.
Q: Can I use the Provisional Degree for job interviews?
Yes, the PDC downloaded from the AKTU ERP is a valid document for job interviews and higher education admissions until the original degree is issued.
