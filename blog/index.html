💡 AKTU Student Helpdesk & FAQ Hub

Search and get instant answers to common AKTU rules, exams, revaluation, grace marks, and backlog questions.

🔍
What is the passing marks criteria in AKTU? ▼
In AKTU, you need to score at least 30% marks in the external end-semester theory exam (30 out of 100 marks, or 21 out of 70 marks) and an overall combined score of 40% (internal sessional marks + external theory exam marks combined) to pass a subject.
What is the Grace Marks rule in AKTU? ▼
AKTU allows up to 10 grace marks per academic year (maximum 5 marks in one semester) to students who are failing in up to 3 subjects, provided they have cleared all other subjects in that year and maintained a minimum annual SGPA of 5.0.
How does AKTU convert CGPA/SGPA to Percentage? ▼
To convert SGPA or CGPA to percentage in AKTU, use the official formula: Percentage = (CGPA - 0.75) * 10. For example, if your CGPA is 8.0, your percentage is (8.0 - 0.75) * 10 = 72.5%. You can use our online converter on the homepage.
How do I apply for AKTU Revaluation / Challenge Evaluation? ▼
You can apply online through your AKTU ERP dashboard within 15-30 days after results are announced. The process consists of Stage 1 (paying Rs. 300 to view your digital answer sheet) and Stage 2 (paying Rs. 2500 for a complete recheck by a different evaluator if you find discrepancy).
What is Year Back (YPT / Year Promotion Tentative) rule in AKTU? ▼
AKTU promotional criteria requires students to clear a minimum credit limit annually. Generally, B.Tech students must clear at least 50% of the total credits offered in the academic year to get promoted to the next year. If you fail to earn these credits, you will get a Year Back (YPT).
How to download AKTU original marksheet or degree certificate? ▼
You can request original degrees, duplicate marksheets, migration certificates, or transcripts through the AKTU Student Service portal on the ERP. After paying the required online fee, the university dispatches the hard copy directly to your college or home address.
What is AKTU One View and how to check results? ▼
AKTU One View is a direct results dashboard (oneview.aktu.ac.in) where any student can check their complete academic record, subject-wise marks, and semester-wise results by just entering their Roll Number. No login password is required.
When are carry-over or backlog exam results declared? ▼
Backlog or carry-over (COP) exam results are typically declared by AKTU 45 to 60 days after the end of the examinations. Once processed, the grades are updated directly inside your AKTU One View portal marksheet list.
