AKTU Challenge Evaluation Stage 1 & Stage 2 Complete Guide

If you got back paper or less marks than expected in AKTU semester result, you can apply for Challenge Evaluation through ERP. Here is the step-by-step breakdown of Stage-1 and Stage-2 process.

📋 Stage 1 vs Stage 2 Comparison & Fees

Phase / Stage Application Fee What You Receive Fee Refund Eligibility
Stage 1 (Copy View) ₹300 per subject Digital PDF copy of evaluated answer booklet sent to your email / ERP dashboard. Non-refundable
Stage 2 (Re-evaluation) ₹2,500 per subject Answer booklet re-evaluated by 2 independent external subject experts. ₹1,500 Refunded if marks increase by 20% or more!

💰 ₹1,500 Fee Refund Rule Explained

If your marks in a 70-mark theory paper increase by 20% (i.e. 14 marks or more) after Stage-2 Challenge Evaluation, AKTU will refund ₹1,500 back to your bank account! Only ₹1,000 is retained by the university as processing fee.
