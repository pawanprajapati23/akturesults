AKTU Result 2026 — Complete Student Guide

Everything you need to know about checking, understanding and acting on your AKTU semester result — from ERP login to revaluation.

1

About AKTU Results 2026

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), formerly known as UPTU, is one of the largest technical universities in India. Every year, AKTU conducts semester examinations for over 8 lakh students across 800+ affiliated colleges in Uttar Pradesh.

The AKTU Result 2026 is awaited by lakhs of B.Tech, MBA, MCA, B.Pharma and other technical students. Results for both odd semester (1st, 3rd, 5th, 7th) and even semester (2nd, 4th, 6th, 8th) are released through the ERP portal and the One View portal. Results are typically declared within 45–60 days of exam completion.

2

How to Check AKTU Result 2026 — Step by Step

Method 1: Via ERP Portal (Full Details)

  1. Visit erp.aktu.ac.in
  2. Enter your enrollment number or university roll number as Username
  3. Enter your Password (contact college if forgotten)
  4. Complete the captcha verification
  5. Click "Login" to enter your student dashboard
  6. Go to Examination → View Result
  7. Select your semester and download or print your result

Method 2: Via One View (No Login Required)

  1. Go to the AKTU One View portal — see direct link guide
  2. Select your Examination Type (Regular / Backlog / Carry Over)
  3. Enter your Roll Number carefully — no spaces
  4. Complete security verification
  5. Click "Show Result" — your marks with SGPA/CGPA appear instantly

AKTU Result 2026 — Important Dates & Schedule

Odd Semester (1st, 3rd, 5th, 7th Sem):

  • Exam Period: December 2025 – January 2026
  • Result Declaration: February – March 2026 — 1st Sem, 3rd Sem, 5th & 7th Sem
  • Back Paper / Supplementary Exams: April – May 2026
  • Revaluation Window: Within 15 days of result — Apply Here

Even Semester (2nd, 4th, 6th, 8th Sem):

  • Exam Period: May – June 2026
  • Result Declaration: July – August 2026 — 2nd, 4th, 6th, 8th Sem
  • Back Paper Exams: September – October 2026

What to Do After AKTU Result?

If You Pass All Subjects ✅

  • Download your result PDF immediately and save multiple copies
  • Verify all marks carefully for any discrepancy
  • Calculate SGPA/CGPA using our free calculator
  • Collect original marksheet from college (ready in 2–3 months)
  • Final year students — start placement preparation right away

If You Have Backlogs 📋

  • Don't panic — backlogs are common and completely clearable
  • Fill the Back Paper Exam Form within the deadline
  • Study from previous year papers — most effective strategy
  • Consult faculty for guidance on difficult subjects

For Revaluation 🔄

  • Apply within 15 days of result — Revaluation Form Guide
  • Fee: approximately ₹300–500 per subject
  • Revaluation results come in 30–45 days

Frequently Asked Questions — AKTU Result 2026

When will AKTU Result 2026 be declared?▾

AKTU typically declares results within 45–60 days after examination completion. Odd semester results: February–March 2026. Even semester results: July–August 2026. Exact dates are officially announced by the university.

Can I check AKTU result without login?▾

Yes! Use the AKTU One View portal. You only need your roll number — no ERP password required. This is the easiest way to check results, especially when ERP is slow or down.

What is the difference between SGPA and CGPA?▾

SGPA = performance in a single semester. CGPA = overall performance across all completed semesters. Use our CGPA Calculator to compute both instantly.

How can I download my AKTU digital marksheet?▾

Download from ERP portal after result declaration. Go to Examination → View Result → Download PDF. Physical marksheets are issued by college after 2–3 months.

What is the minimum attendance required in AKTU?▾

AKTU requires minimum 75% attendance in each subject to sit in semester examinations. Use our Attendance Calculator to track your status.

What to do if AKTU result shows an error?▾

Verify your roll number has no extra spaces or wrong digits. If result shows incorrect marks, immediately contact your college examination cell. You can also apply for revaluation within 15 days.
