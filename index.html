AKTU Result 2026 — Complete Student Guide

Everything you need to know about checking, understanding and acting on your AKTU semester result — from ERP login to revaluation, grading system to career planning.

About AKTU & Its Examination System

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), formerly known as Uttar Pradesh Technical University (UPTU), is one of the largest technical universities in India. Established in 2000 and renamed in 2015 in honour of India's beloved scientist and former President, AKTU is headquartered in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh. Every year, AKTU conducts semester examinations for over 8 lakh students across 800+ affiliated colleges spanning the entire state of Uttar Pradesh.

The university offers undergraduate and postgraduate programmes including B.Tech, B.Pharma, MBA, MCA, M.Tech, BHMCT and several other technical and management courses. The examination process is conducted twice a year — once for odd semesters (1st, 3rd, 5th, 7th) in November–December, and once for even semesters (2nd, 4th, 6th, 8th) in May–June. The AKTU Result 2026 is awaited by millions of students and their families, as it determines academic progression, scholarship eligibility, and placement opportunities.

AKTU has undergone significant digital transformation in recent years. The introduction of the ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) portal and the One View portal has made result checking far more convenient than the older manual processes. Students can now view detailed marksheets, download digital certificates, track attendance, and access all academic records from a single online platform.

📌 Did You Know? AKTU is among the top 5 largest technical universities in India by student enrollment. Its affiliated colleges produce over 2 lakh engineering graduates every year, making it one of the most significant contributors to India's technical workforce.

How to Check AKTU Result 2026 — Step by Step

AKTU provides two official methods to check your semester result. Both are free, require no special software, and work on mobile and desktop browsers. Here is a complete guide for both methods:

Method 1: Via ERP Portal (Detailed Marksheet with Internals)

The ERP portal gives you access to your complete marksheet, including internal assessment marks, attendance records, fee payment history and all academic documents. This is the recommended method for downloading your official result.

  1. Visit erp.aktu.ac.in on any browser
  2. Enter your Enrollment Number or University Roll Number as your Username
  3. Enter your Password — if forgotten, use "Forgot Password" or contact your college's exam cell
  4. Complete the CAPTCHA security verification
  5. Click the blue "Login" button to enter your student dashboard
  6. From the top menu, navigate to Examination → View Result
  7. Select the desired Semester and Academic Year
  8. Your complete marksheet appears — click Download PDF to save it
⚠️ ERP Server Overload: During result declaration days, the ERP portal often becomes slow or temporarily unavailable due to millions of students accessing it simultaneously. If you face issues, try again during off-peak hours (early morning or late night), or use Method 2 (One View) as a quick alternative.

Method 2: Via One View Portal (No Login Required)

The AKTU One View portal is the quickest way to check your result. You only need your Roll Number — no ERP password required. This is especially useful if you have forgotten your ERP credentials or if the main ERP portal is slow.

  1. Go to the AKTU One View portal — see our direct link guide
  2. Select your Examination Type — Regular, Backlog (Ex-Student), or Carry Over
  3. Choose your Programme (B.Tech, MBA, MCA, B.Pharma, etc.)
  4. Select the Course Year and Semester
  5. Enter your Roll Number carefully — avoid spaces or wrong characters
  6. Complete the security verification or CAPTCHA
  7. Click "Show Result" — your subject-wise marks, SGPA and CGPA appear instantly
  8. Take a screenshot or print the page for future reference
💡 Pro Tip: Bookmark both the ERP portal and One View portal link on your phone before the result day. During peak traffic, having the pages pre-loaded can save valuable time. Our One View guide page always has the most current working link.

AKTU Grading System & Marking Scheme 2026

AKTU follows a 10-point grading scale based on the Choice Based Credit System (CBCS). Understanding the grading scheme is essential for calculating your SGPA and CGPA accurately. Use our free CGPA Calculator to convert marks to grades instantly.

Passing Criteria: A student must score at least 35% in the external (end-semester) examination AND at least 40% in the combined total (internal + external marks) to pass a subject. Failing to meet either of these conditions results in a backlog in that subject.

Grade Marks Range Grade Points Description
O 90–100 10 Outstanding
A+ 80–89 9 Excellent
A 70–79 8 Very Good
B+ 60–69 7 Good
B 50–59 6 Above Average
C 40–49 5 Average
P 35–39 4 Pass
F Below 35 0 Fail — Backlog

How SGPA is Calculated: SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average) = Sum of (Credit × Grade Point for each subject) ÷ Total Credits in that semester. For example, if you have 5 subjects each worth 4 credits and you score grade points of 8, 9, 7, 8, 9 — your SGPA = (4×8 + 4×9 + 4×7 + 4×8 + 4×9) ÷ 20 = 164 ÷ 20 = 8.2 SGPA.

How CGPA is Calculated: CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) = Sum of (SGPA × Total Credits for each semester) ÷ Total Credits completed across all semesters. Use our free AKTU CGPA Calculator to compute this instantly without manual calculation.

Converting CGPA to Percentage: As per AKTU's official formula — Percentage = (CGPA − 0.5) × 10. So a CGPA of 7.5 equals approximately 70% marks.

AKTU ERP Login — Complete Guide for Students

The AKTU ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) portal at erp.aktu.ac.in is the official student portal for Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University. It is the single most important online resource for every AKTU student. Here is everything you need to know about using it effectively:

What Can You Do on AKTU ERP?

  • View and download your semester marksheets (external + internal marks)
  • Check your overall SGPA and CGPA across all completed semesters
  • Download your admit card before each examination
  • Fill and submit your exam forms (regular and back paper)
  • Check attendance records subject-wise
  • Apply for revaluation or re-totaling after result declaration
  • Access fee payment history and pending dues
  • View important university notifications and circulars
  • Download digital copies of migration certificate, TC, and other documents

Common ERP Login Problems & Solutions:

  • Forgot Password: Click "Forgot Password" on the ERP login page and enter your registered email or mobile number to reset it. If that fails, contact your college's examination department — they can reset it internally.
  • Account Locked: Multiple wrong password attempts can temporarily lock your account. Wait 30 minutes and try again, or contact the college exam cell.
  • Portal Not Opening: This usually happens due to server overload on result days. Try using a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge), clear cache, or check the site during off-peak hours.
  • Captcha Not Loading: Refresh the page, disable browser extensions, or try incognito/private mode.
  • Result Not Visible: If you can log in but your result is not showing, it may not be declared for your specific semester yet. Keep checking — results for different batches/programmes may come at different times.
💡 Mobile Tip: The ERP portal works best on Chrome for Android and Safari for iOS. If you face display issues, switch to "Desktop Site" mode in your mobile browser settings for better access to all features.

What to Do After AKTU Result 2026?

Your AKTU result is not just a scorecard — it is a roadmap for your next steps. Depending on your result, here is exactly what you should do:

If You Pass All Subjects ✅

  • Immediately download and save your result PDF from the ERP portal — save multiple copies in Google Drive and your phone
  • Verify every subject's marks carefully — match them against your OMR sheets and answer booklets if you remember your performance
  • Calculate your SGPA and CGPA using our free AKTU calculator to understand your academic standing
  • Collect your original marksheet from your college — typically available 2 to 3 months after result declaration
  • Final year students should immediately start or intensify placement preparation — this is the most important thing you can do right after passing your final semester
  • Check if you qualify for any merit scholarships based on your CGPA
  • Register for the next semester's exam form if available

If You Have Backlogs 📋

  • Do not panic — backlogs are extremely common in AKTU. Thousands of students carry backlogs and still go on to have successful careers
  • Note exactly which subjects you have failed and their subject codes
  • Fill the Back Paper Exam Form as soon as it becomes available — missing the deadline means waiting another full semester
  • Download previous year question papers for your backlog subjects from our previous year papers section — this is the most effective study strategy for back exams
  • Create a focused study schedule for only the backlog subjects — do not spread yourself thin
  • Consult faculty members or seniors who have cleared the same subjects
  • Check if there is a cap on allowed backlogs for your programme's placement eligibility — many companies require zero backlogs at the time of joining

For Revaluation 🔄

  • If you feel your marks do not reflect your actual performance, apply for revaluation within 15 days of result declaration — Revaluation Form Guide
  • The revaluation fee is approximately ₹300–500 per subject — payable online through the ERP portal
  • Revaluation results are typically declared within 30 to 45 days
  • You can also apply for re-totaling (checking for calculation errors) which is cheaper and faster than full revaluation
  • Marks can go up or remain the same after revaluation — they typically cannot go below the original score

Semester-Wise AKTU B.Tech Result Overview

Every semester of your AKTU B.Tech journey has its own characteristics, difficulty level and importance. Here is a quick overview of what each semester result means for your academic and professional future:

1st Semester Result: The first result is often a shock for many students transitioning from the Class 12 board exam pattern to the university semester system. The 1st semester introduces engineering mathematics, physics, chemistry, and basic engineering subjects. A poor 1st semester result is recoverable — do not be disheartened. Focus on understanding the examination pattern, time management, and internal marks strategy. Check AKTU 1st Semester Result 2026.

3rd Semester Result: By the 3rd semester, core engineering subjects begin. This is when students often encounter their first significant academic challenges. The 3rd semester result is important for maintaining a decent CGPA that supports future scholarship and placement eligibility. Check AKTU 3rd Semester Result 2026.

5th & 7th Semester Results: These are the most placement-critical semesters. Most companies conducting campus recruitment look at CGPA up to the 6th or 7th semester. Performing well in the 5th and 7th semester can significantly boost your CGPA and placement opportunities. Check AKTU 5th & 7th Semester Result 2026.

Even Semester Results (2nd, 4th, 6th, 8th): Even semester results are typically declared in July–August and carry equal weight in CGPA calculation. The 8th semester result marks the completion of your B.Tech degree — a major milestone. Results for all even semesters can be found in our B.Tech Results section.

📊 CGPA for Placements: Most top companies (TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Accenture, etc.) require a minimum CGPA of 6.0 or 6.5. Premium companies often require 7.0+ CGPA. Government jobs and higher education (M.Tech, MBA) may require 7.5+ CGPA. Plan accordingly from the 1st semester itself.

How to Improve Your AKTU CGPA — Practical Tips

Your CGPA is a cumulative measure — every semester contributes to it. The earlier you start performing well, the higher your overall CGPA will be. Here are actionable strategies that AKTU toppers consistently follow:

  • Maximize Internal Marks: Internal assessments (sessionals, assignments, lab work, MST) contribute 30–40% of your total marks in most subjects. These are often easier to score high in than external exams. Never skip internals — a zero in internals forces you to score very high in externals just to pass.
  • Focus on Previous Year Papers: AKTU exam questions follow highly predictable patterns. Solving the last 5–7 years' question papers for each subject is the single most effective exam preparation strategy. Download them from our Previous Year Papers page.
  • Understand the Syllabus Coverage: AKTU external papers often focus on specific units more than others. Study the official syllabus carefully and prioritize high-weightage units.
  • Attend All Classes & Maintain 75% Attendance: AKTU mandates 75% attendance for exam eligibility. Missing classes not only risks your exam eligibility but also results in you missing important topics directly relevant to exams. Use our Attendance Calculator to track your status.
  • Clear Backlogs Immediately: Do not carry backlogs semester after semester. Each uncleared backlog brings your CGPA down because failed subjects contribute 0 grade points. Clear them in the very next back exam opportunity.
  • Use AKTU Study Materials: The Quantum Series, official AKTU notes, and study materials are specifically designed for the AKTU examination pattern. They are more relevant than generic textbooks for scoring in university exams. Download from our Quantum PDF section.
  • Form Study Groups: Collaborative learning is highly effective, especially for mathematical and analytical subjects. Study groups help you identify and fill knowledge gaps faster.

AKTU One View Portal — Everything You Need to Know

The AKTU One View Portal is a public-facing result checking system maintained by AKTU. Unlike the ERP portal which requires student login credentials, One View is accessible to anyone with just a roll number. It is the fastest and most accessible way to check AKTU results — especially useful when:

  • You have forgotten your ERP password and cannot recover it before results come out
  • The ERP portal is down or overloaded due to result traffic
  • You want to quickly check results of a friend, sibling or classmate
  • You need a quick result screenshot for WhatsApp or sharing without downloading a PDF
  • Parents want to check results without navigating the full ERP system

The One View portal displays subject-wise marks, grade points, SGPA for the current semester, and cumulative CGPA. However, it does not show internal assessment breakdowns or detailed attendance records — those are only visible on the full ERP portal.

Frequently Asked Questions — AKTU Result 2026

When will AKTU Result 2026 be declared?▾

AKTU typically declares results within 45–60 days after examination completion. Odd semester results are expected in February–March 2026. Even semester results are expected in July–August 2026. Exact dates are officially announced by the university closer to declaration time. Bookmark this page for real-time updates.

Can I check AKTU result without login?▾

Yes! Use the AKTU One View portal. You only need your roll number — no ERP password required. It shows subject-wise marks, SGPA and CGPA instantly. This is the easiest way to check results, especially when ERP is slow or down.

What is the difference between SGPA and CGPA?▾

SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average) measures your performance in a single semester only. CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) reflects your overall academic performance across all completed semesters. CGPA is what employers and graduate schools look at. Use our AKTU CGPA Calculator to compute both instantly.

How can I download my AKTU digital marksheet?▾

Log in to erp.aktu.ac.in with your student credentials. Navigate to Examination → View Result, select your semester, and click the Download PDF button. The digital marksheet is an official university document and is accepted for most academic and employment purposes. Physical marksheets are issued by your college 2–3 months after result declaration.

What is the minimum attendance required for AKTU exams?▾

AKTU requires a minimum of 75% attendance in each subject to be eligible to appear in semester examinations. Students with attendance between 60–75% may be granted conditional eligibility with a written application and valid reasons. Students below 60% are typically not allowed to sit for exams in that subject. Use our Attendance Calculator to know your current status.

How to apply for AKTU revaluation 2026?▾

Revaluation applications must be submitted within 15 days of result declaration through the ERP portal under the Examination section. The fee is approximately ₹300–500 per subject. Revaluation results are typically declared within 30–45 days. You can also apply for re-totaling (checking totaling errors only) which is faster and cheaper. Read our full Revaluation Guide for step-by-step instructions.

What to do if AKTU result shows an error or incorrect marks?▾

First, verify your roll number carefully — even a single digit error can show another student's result. If your result genuinely shows incorrect marks, immediately contact your college examination cell with your answer booklet's barcode number if you have it. You should also apply for revaluation within the 15-day window. Do not wait — the window closes quickly and you cannot apply for revaluation after the deadline.

Does AKTU allow more than 2 backlogs for placement?▾

AKTU itself does not set placement eligibility criteria — that is determined by individual companies recruiting from your college. Most major IT companies like TCS, Infosys, and Wipro allow up to 2 active backlogs during recruitment but require zero backlogs at the time of joining. Premium companies typically require zero backlogs throughout. Always check the specific eligibility criteria of each company you apply to, and aim to clear all backlogs as soon as possible.
