📋 Official AKTU Management Quota Eligibility Rules

  • Candidates must have passed Class 12th Examination with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects.
  • Minimum aggregate marks in PCM (Physics, Chemistry, Maths) must be 50% for General/OBC and 45% for SC/ST.
  • Direct management quota seats are sanctioned up to 15% of total approved intake as per AKTU ordinances.
