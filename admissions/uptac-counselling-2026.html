💡 Freeze vs Float: What Should You Choose?

Option 1: FREEZE - Select Freeze if you are 100% satisfied with the seat allotted in Round 1/2. You accept this college/branch and will not participate in future rounds.

Option 2: FLOAT - Select Float if you want to accept the current seat as a backup, but want to upgrade to a higher preference choice in Round 2 or Round 3!

💰 Seat Acceptance Fee & Refund Policy

  • General / OBC Candidates: ₹20,000 (Adjustable in 1st year college tuition fee).
  • SC / ST Candidates: ₹12,000 (Adjustable in 1st year college tuition fee).
  • Withdrawal / Refund: If you withdraw before Round 3 specified deadline, partial refund (after ₹2,000 deduction) is credited back to your bank account.
