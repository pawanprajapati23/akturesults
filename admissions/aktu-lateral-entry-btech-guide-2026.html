Complete Overview: AKTU B.Tech Lateral Entry 2026

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), Lucknow, offers direct admission to the 2nd year (3rd semester) of its 4-year Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) degree programs across all affiliated government and private engineering institutes in Uttar Pradesh. This pathway, officially designated as B.Tech Lateral Entry, is designed for candidates who have completed a 3-year Polytechnic Engineering Diploma or a 3-year B.Sc Degree with Mathematics.

Lateral Entry enables students to save one full academic year, entering directly into core branch subjects alongside regular 2nd-year engineering cohorts. All admissions are conducted transparently through the Uttar Pradesh Technical Admission Counselling (UPTAC) based on merit scores obtained in the National Testing Agency (NTA) CUET UG Examination.

📌 Important AICTE & AKTU Lateral Entry Guideline 2026

According to AICTE regulations, 10% supernumerary seats over and above the sanctioned 1st-year intake are reserved exclusively for Lateral Entry admissions. Additionally, any vacant unfilled seats from the previous academic year's 1st-year batch are added to the lateral entry seat matrix during UPTAC counselling.

Comprehensive Eligibility Criteria 2026

Candidates must fulfill specific academic standards set by AKTU and AICTE to qualify for UPTAC Lateral Entry counselling:

1. For Polytechnic Diploma Holders

  • Qualification: Passed 3-year AICTE-approved Diploma examination in any branch of Engineering & Technology from UP Board of Technical Education (BTEUP) or any recognized state technical education board.
  • Minimum Marks: Obtained at least 45% aggregate marks (40% in case of candidates belonging to SC/ST reserved categories of Uttar Pradesh).
  • Branch Eligibility: Diploma holders can opt for any engineering discipline (e.g. Mechanical Diploma holders can opt for CSE, ECE, or Civil in B.Tech).

2. For B.Sc Graduates

  • Qualification: Passed B.Sc degree from a UGC-recognized university with a minimum of 45% marks (40% for SC/ST).
  • Mandatory Subject: Passed Class 10+2 (Intermediate) with Mathematics as a core subject.
  • Bridge Course Requirement: B.Sc students must pass mandatory remedial subjects in Engineering Graphics / Drawing and Engineering Mechanics during their 2nd year of B.Tech.

UPTAC CUET UG 2026 Exam Pattern & Syllabus

Admission to AKTU B.Tech Lateral Entry is governed entirely by CUET UG test scores. Candidates must carefully select the correct domain and general papers during NTA CUET registration:

Step-by-Step UPTAC Counselling Procedure 2026

1

CUET UG Exam & Result

Appear for NTA CUET UG domain test and download official scorecard with percentile scores.

2

UPTAC Registration

Register online at uptac.admissions.nic.in, pay counselling fee (₹1,000), and upload documents.

3

Document Verification

Online verification of diploma marksheet, category certificate, sub-category, and domicile certificate by UPTAC team.

4

Choice Filling & Choice Locking

Use our UPTAC Choice Filling Predictor to arrange choices in order of college preference and lock them.

5

Seat Allotment & Reporting

Download seat allotment letter, pay Seat Acceptance Fee (₹20,000 / ₹12,000), and complete physical reporting at college.

Required Documents Checklist for Verification

Ensure you prepare two sets of self-attested photocopies along with original documents for physical reporting:

  1. Class 10th (High School) Marksheet & Passing Certificate (for Date of Birth proof).
  2. 3-Year Polytechnic Engineering Diploma Marksheets (All Semesters) & Final Certificate OR B.Sc Final Marksheet.
  3. CUET UG 2026 Admit Card & NTA Official Score Card.
  4. UPTAC Counselling Registration & Seat Allotment Confirmation Letter.
  5. Category Certificate (OBC-NCL / SC / ST / EWS) issued on or after April 1, 2026 by competent UP authority.
  6. Domicile Certificate of Uttar Pradesh (if candidate passed qualifying diploma outside UP).
  7. Character Certificate from last attended institution and Transfer / Migration Certificate.
  8. Medical Fitness Certificate (as per UPTAC Format given in Information Brochure).
  9. Income Certificate (for Fee Waiver seat applicants or UP Post-Matric Scholarship eligibility).
  10. Passport-size photographs (6 copies) & Aadhaar Card photocopy.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Can outside UP (Other State) students apply for AKTU Lateral Entry? ▼
Yes. While government colleges reserve 100% seats for UP Domicile candidates, private AKTU colleges permit non-UP domicile diploma holders to secure lateral entry seats under open management quota and vacant seats during spot rounds.
Is UP Scholarship applicable for B.Tech Lateral Entry students? ▼
Yes! Eligible SC/ST/OBC/EWS candidates from Uttar Pradesh pursuing B.Tech Lateral Entry can apply for the UP Post-Matric Scholarship & Fee Reimbursement Scheme through Saksham portal (Read AKTU Scholarship Guide).
Can I change my branch during B.Tech Lateral Entry? ▼
Yes, as per updated AICTE rules, a diploma holder in Civil or Mechanical can take admission into CSE or IT in B.Tech 2nd year. However, branch change after taking admission in 2nd year is not allowed.
What is the passing criteria for B.Sc bridge subjects in 2nd year? ▼
B.Sc graduates must pass Engineering Graphics and Engineering Mechanics papers conducted by AKTU in 3rd/4th semester with a minimum of 30% marks. These subjects are qualifying in nature and do not affect CGPA calculation.
