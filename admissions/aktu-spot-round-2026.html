Key Features of AKTU Admissions

  • Admissions to 800+ affiliated colleges across UP
  • Multiple entrance exams accepted (JEE, UPSEE, CUET)
  • Online counselling and seat allotment process
  • Category-wise reservations (SC/ST/OBC/EWS)
  • Transparent and merit-based admission system
  • Multiple counselling rounds for better opportunities

Admission Process Steps

  • Step 1: Register for counselling on official portal
  • Step 2: Fill personal and academic details
  • Step 3: Upload required documents
  • Step 4: Pay counselling registration fee
  • Step 5: Fill college and branch preferences
  • Step 6: Check seat allotment result
  • Step 7: Accept seat and report to college
  • Step 8: Complete document verification
  • Step 9: Pay admission fees

Required Documents

  • High School (10th) Marksheet & Certificate
  • Intermediate (12th) Marksheet & Certificate
  • Entrance Exam Scorecard (JEE/UPSEE/CUET)
  • AKTU Counselling Allotment Letter
  • Caste Certificate (if applicable)
  • Income Certificate (for EWS/Fee Waiver)
  • Domicile Certificate (UP Domicile)
  • Aadhar Card
  • Passport Size Photographs
  • Transfer Certificate from Previous Institution
  • Character Certificate
  • Migration Certificate (if from other board)
💡 Important: Keep both original and self-attested photocopies of all documents. Original documents will be verified and returned.

Eligibility Criteria

For B.Tech Admission:

  • Passed 12th with Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics
  • Minimum 45% marks in PCM (40% for reserved categories)
  • Valid JEE Main or UPSEE score
  • Age limit as per AICTE norms

For MBA Admission:

  • Bachelor's degree in any discipline
  • Minimum 50% marks in graduation (45% for reserved)
  • Valid UPSEE or other accepted entrance exam score

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get admission without entrance exam?

No, you need a valid score in accepted entrance exams like JEE Main, UPSEE, or CUET for AKTU admissions.

How many colleges can I apply for?

You can fill unlimited college and branch preferences during choice filling in the counselling process.

What if I don't get seat in first round?

You can participate in subsequent counselling rounds. AKTU conducts multiple rounds until seats are filled.

Is AKTU admission open for all India students?

Yes, students from all states can apply, but UP domicile students get preference under state quota.
