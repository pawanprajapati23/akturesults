AKTU UP Scholarship 2026: Complete Fee Reimbursement & Online Status Tracker Guide

Uttar Pradesh Social Welfare Department (Samaj Kalyan Vibhag) offers financial assistance and tuition fee reimbursement up to ₹50,000 to ₹1,20,000 per year for eligible B.Tech, MBA, MCA, B.Pharm, B.Arch, and Diploma students studying in AKTU affiliated government and private institutions.

⚠️ 5 Critical Rules to Prevent UP Scholarship Rejection in AKTU

  • 1. NPCI Aadhaar Bank Account Seeding: Your savings bank account MUST be mapped with Aadhaar on the NPCI server. Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) will fail if NPCI seeding is missing! Check status on the official UIDAI website.
  • 2. Minimum 75% Attendance on AKTU ERP: Your college Registrar updates your attendance directly on the AKTU ERP portal. If attendance is less than 75%, your form will be rejected at the institute level.
  • 3. Income Certificate Verification: Income certificates must be issued after April 1st of the current financial year and verified on the edistrict.up.gov.in portal.
  • 4. Enrollment Number & Roll Number Matching: Enter your exact AKTU University Enrollment Number (for 2nd, 3rd, 4th year) or Counseling Allotment Number (for 1st year).
  • 5. Non-Refundable Fee Exact Match: Enter the exact Tuition Fee amount mentioned on your official institute fee receipt (excluding hostel and caution money).

📝 Step-by-Step Application & Renewal Procedure for 2026

  1. Visit scholarship.up.gov.in and click Student → Registration → Postmatric Other Than Inter.
  2. Fill Fresh Registration details (Class 10th Roll Number, Passing Year, Board, Mobile number linked to Aadhaar).
  3. Log in with your Registration Number, Password, and Date of Birth.
  4. Fill personal details, caste certificate number, income certificate number, and AKTU course enrollment details.
  5. Upload passport size photo, scanned marksheets, and bank passbook first page.
  6. Submit form for Institutional Verification and print 3 hard copies.
  7. Submit printed form with attached fee receipts and certificates to your college Scholarship Department before the deadline.

📂 Documents Required for Scholarship Application Submission

Make sure to attach self-attested photocopies of the following documents along with your printed hard copy application form:

  • High School (10th) & Intermediate (12th) Marksheets & Passing Certificates
  • All Previous Semester AKTU Marksheets (For 2nd, 3rd, 4th Year Renewal Students)
  • UPTAC College Seat Allotment Letter & Latest Year Fee Receipts
  • Valid UP Domicile Certificate & Caste Certificate (OBC/SC/ST)
  • Valid Income Certificate verified on edistrict.up.gov.in
  • Aadhaar Card copy & Bank Passbook copy showing NPCI Active Seeding
  • Affidavit for Gap Year (If there is any drop year between 12th and college)

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (UP Scholarship AKTU)

1. What if my bank account is not linked with Aadhaar NPCI?

Visit your bank branch immediately and submit an Aadhaar NPCI Seeding Form. Without NPCI mapping, DBT payment cannot be processed by District Social Welfare Office.

2. Can lateral entry B.Tech students apply for UP Scholarship?

Yes! B.Tech 2nd year Lateral Entry students (Diploma / B.Sc passout) are eligible under Postmatric Other Than Inter category.

3. How to check UP Scholarship Status Online 2026?

Log in to scholarship.up.gov.in, click on Status → Check Application Status 2026 to view current verification progress at Institute, PFMS, and District Committee levels.

4. What is the deadline to submit UP Scholarship form in college?

The online registration generally opens in August-September and closes by October-November. Check the official portal for current phase deadlines.
