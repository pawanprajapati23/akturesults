💰 UP SCHOLARSHIP 2026 GUIDE

AKTU UP Scholarship 2026: Fee Reimbursement Amount & Status Check

Uttar Pradesh Social Welfare Department reimburses college tuition fees up to ₹50,000 to ₹1,20,000/year for eligible B.Tech, MBA, MCA, B.Pharm, and Diploma students studying in AKTU affiliated colleges.

⚠️ Top Mandatory Rules to Prevent Scholarship Rejection

  • 1. NPCI Bank Aadhaar Seeding: Your bank account MUST be mapped with Aadhaar on the NPCI server. Without NPCI seeding, Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) funds fail!
  • 2. 75% ERP Attendance Rule: AKTU college attendance marked on the ERP portal must be 75% or higher.
  • 3. Income Certificate Verification: Income certificate must be issued in the name of Father/Mother and verified on the e-district UP portal.
  • 4. Non-Refundable Fee Matching: Enter exact tuition fee amount as per institute admission slip (excluding hostel and caution deposit).

📝 Step-by-Step Online Application Process

  1. Visit scholarship.up.gov.in and click Student → Registration → Postmatric Other Than Inter.
  2. Fill Registration details (10th roll number, Board, Mobile number linked to Aadhaar).
  3. Login with Application ID and Password. Fill academic details, enrollment number, and institute fee receipt details.
  4. Upload passport photo, caste certificate, and income certificate.
  5. Submit form for Institutional Verification and print 3 hard copies.
  6. Submit printed form with attached fee receipts to your college Registrar / Scholarship Cell.
