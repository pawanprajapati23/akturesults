🎓 AKTU Degree, Migration & Provisional Certificate Apply Online

Official step-by-step process to apply online for Provisional Degree Certificates, Migration Certificates, Degree Dispatch by Speed Post, and Duplicate Marksheets.

💰 Official AKTU Certificate Fee Schedule

1
Visit the AKTU Student Service (SS) Portal

Navigate to official AKTU portal at https://aktu.ac.in and click on Student Services or direct URL https://erp.aktu.ac.in/WebPages/StudentServices/StudentDashboard.aspx.

2
Enter Roll Number & OTP Verification

Enter your 12-digit AKTU Roll Number and registered mobile number / email ID. An OTP will be dispatched to verify your identity.

3
Select Required Certificate & Fill Postal Address

Select the document you need (e.g. Provisional Degree or Migration Certificate). Ensure your postal address, PIN code, and mobile number are 100% accurate for Speed Post delivery.

4
Pay e-Challan Fee & Track Reference Number

Make online payment using UPI, Net Banking, or Debit Card. Note down the generated Token Reference Number (e.g. SS-2026-XXXXX) to track dispatch status.

Important Guidelines for AKTU Alumni & Final Year Students

  • Zero Pending Backlog Requirement: You cannot apply for a Provisional Degree or Original Degree if you have any uncleared carryover (COP) paper.
  • Name Correction in Hindi/English: If there is a spelling mistake in your name or father's name on your marksheet, apply for Correction in Name with a copy of your 10th High School marksheet.
  • Speed Post Tracking: Once dispatched from AKTU Lucknow central store, the India Post Consignment Tracking ID will be updated on your Student Dashboard.
